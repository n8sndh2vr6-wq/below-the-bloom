INT. MAW TERRITORY – CAVERNOUS DEPTHS

Darkness. Silence. Then—

FWOOOM.

A soft purple light pulses through the cave, crawling across jagged walls and down endless chasms. It rolls like fog, eerie and unnatural. It illuminates them—Maws. Dozens. Slithering and twitching on the walls and ceiling. Their mouths half-open. Their eyes glowing orange.

OYSTER (whispers to himself)
Okay… stay low. Stay very low…

*He drags himself along the rocks, staying behind cover. The soft glow from the purple light reveals sharp angles and long, twisted shadows. The Maws begin clicking—echolocation.

CHIP (faintly over comms)
Oyster? You reading this? I’m—static—I’m in a narrow trench. Movement everywhere. Can’t see you.

OYSTER (tense whisper)
I hear you. Stay put. I’ll come to you.

A Maw suddenly jerks its head toward the sound. Oyster freezes. The creature clicks again, tilting its head, before scurrying away. He moves quickly through a cluster of tall, broken spires, ducking under a hanging rock.

One of the Maws passes right above him, its tendrils dripping.

CHIP (distorted)
You sure about this? They’re hunting like a pack. Like wolves with sonar.

OYSTER (whispers)
Then I’ll be the shadow in the trees.

He climbs toward a higher ledge, purple light still pulsing through the cavern. He sees a flicker—CHIP’S projector light flashing faintly on a wall up ahead.

OYSTER
There you are.

He moves closer—but then—

CRACK.

The rock beneath his hand gives way.

OYSTER
Oh—NO—

He slips—then the entire ledge collapses beneath him. He falls.

OYSTER (screaming)
CHIIIIIP—!

The purple light follows him as he plummets into the abyss, alone.

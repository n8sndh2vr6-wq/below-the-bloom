INT. CRASHED SHIP – SUBMERGED INTERIOR – DIMLY LIT – MOMENTS AFTER IMPACT

Flickering lights. Alarms hum faintly. Water drips from above. OYSTER lies unconscious, floating just above the floor in low gravity. His eyes twitch open. A soft voice echoes in his helmet.

CHIP (V.O.)
Oyster. Oyster. Oyster wake up—this is officially outside my expertise in nap timing.

OYSTER gasps, jolting awake. He scrambles up, disoriented. His pressure suit hisses softly as it auto-seals a minor tear.

OYSTER
Wha—what happened…?

CHIP
You got smacked by cosmic weirdness and decided a metal floor was a good place to sleep. We crashed. Whole ship. Systems are offline. We’re alive. Technically. Congratulations.

OYSTER (blinking hard, looking around)
The others…?

CHIP (somberly)
Most of the biosignatures are gone, or… way too faint. I think we’re it.

OYSTER (quietly)
No. No no no…

He pushes himself toward a cracked viewport. Outside is endless water. A dim purple glow pulses far off in the depths. OYSTER stares.

OYSTER (softly)
It was that thing. That glow.

CHIP
Yeah. Color me shocked that flying through an alien ocean lit up by demonic jellybean light didn’t end well.

OYSTER
The agency. They were tracking this planet for years. Thought it was just another Europa knockoff. But something’s here, Chip. Something huge.

CHIP (projecting on his helmet now)
You think this has anything to do with that “structure” file you obsessed over? The one buried under ten layers of classified nonsense?

OYSTER (nodding)
The castle. The deep structure. The thing even the agency only had blurry scans of. They said it was old, maybe even alive.

CHIP
You’re suggesting we what—go sightseeing? In an alien trench? With a knife and a glorified water gun?

OYSTER (pulling his mask tighter, suiting up)
I’m suggesting we do the only thing we can. We find it. The king they talked about, the one who can send us home… if that’s real, that’s our only way out of this.

CHIP
Just to be clear—you’re trusting legends you read in half-corrupted agency logs written by people who went missing?

OYSTER
Yeah. Pretty much.

CHIP (long pause)
Okay. Fine. But I’m logging a formal complaint to the universe.

OYSTER grabs a small oxygen blaster and his scanner from a locker, hooks CHIP into his shoulder projector port. The ship creaks ominously.

OYSTER (heading to the airlock)
You coming?

CHIP (dryly)
No. I’ll stay in the haunted wreck with the corpses. Of course I’m coming.

OYSTER (half-smiling)
Let’s go find the crown.

The airlock hisses open. Beyond it lies the freezing, alien abyss. They plunge into the water, vanishing into the dark.

CUT TO BLACK.

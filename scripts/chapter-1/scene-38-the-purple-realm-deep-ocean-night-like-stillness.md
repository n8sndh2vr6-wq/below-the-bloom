EXT. THE PURPLE REALM – DEEP OCEAN – NIGHT-LIKE STILLNESS

OYSTER and CHIP float in the eerie beauty of the purple abyss. The BLOOM drifts before them in the open water — calm, regal, and smiling faintly.

THE BLOOM
(Silky voice)
You know… I haven’t felt a proper smile in years. Not since I learned what joy really is.
It’s struggle. Desperation. Watching hope stretch thin like gill-wire.
So let’s play a game.

OYSTER tenses, his hand ready over the oxygen pressure blaster.

OYSTER
You’re insane.

THE BLOOM
(Chuckling)
No, no, no. I’m entertaining. Here are the rules.
One by one, I’ll send a creature to fight you. You defeat it — it joins your side. Simple.
But the further you go… the more you face at once. Stronger, faster, angrier.
And yes… the kings come last.
Win, and maybe I’ll let you live. Lose, and… well, fate wins again.

CHIP
(Pinging nervously)
This doesn’t sound like statistically favorable entertainment.

OYSTER
We don’t get a choice, do we?

THE BLOOM
(Smiling wider)
Correct.

Without another word, the water shifts — a massive FROSTSTALKER barrels out of the fog, eyes glowing purple.

INT. UNDERWATER CAVE – NIGHT

The cave is calm, filtered with blue light from distant currents. Oyster sits on a smooth rock, removing the oxygen blaster from his hip. Steam rolls off his suit. Chip hovers nearby, projecting a soft orange glow onto the cave walls—graphs and signal readings flicker briefly, then vanish.

CHIP:
The seismic tremors have calmed. Looks like the Froststalkers are holding their ground for now.

OYSTER (grinning):
Well, good. I’d hate to have crawled through frozen death soup just to get eaten politely.

CHIP:
Their king showed remarkable restraint. Especially considering your blaster was half-raised the entire conversation.

OYSTER (shrugs):
Habit. Agency drilled that in. “Don’t trust anything that has teeth longer than your forearm.” Guess that includes half this planet.

CHIP:
And yet, here you are—making friends with monsters. Progress.

OYSTER:
Don’t get all sentimental on me. You’re still just a talking flash drive.

CHIP (mock offended):
Excuse you. I am a Class-V synthetic cognition array equipped with adaptive—
(Oyster raises an eyebrow)
—Yeah, okay. Talking flash drive.

(Beat of silence. The water outside the cave pulses gently. Peaceful, for once.)

OYSTER (quieter):
You think the agency’s freaking out right now?

CHIP:
They lost signal three days ago. Full radio silence. Based on previous logs… yeah. They’re panicking. Probably blaming it on you.

OYSTER:
Figures. Sixteen-year-old crash test dummy survives alien sea hellscape—must be his fault.

CHIP:
Statistically, it is impressive. You’ve evaded multiple apex predators, made contact with sentient life, and only insulted your AI companion seventeen times today.

OYSTER (smirking):
Eighteen. You don’t count sarcasm well.

CHIP:
Noted. Adding “rude” to your psychological profile.

(They sit in the quiet again. A low, distant echo sounds from deeper in the trench—Oyster tenses, but Chip doesn’t react.)

CHIP (softer):
…They’ll come for you. Maybe not now, but they will.

OYSTER:
Let ‘em. I’m not done yet.

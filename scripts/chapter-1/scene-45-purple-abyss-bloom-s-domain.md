INT. PURPLE ABYSS – BLOOM’S DOMAIN

The ocean churns with power. The BLOOM hovers before them, its fins stretching and warping into twin, blade-like appendages, each as large as the FROSTSTALKER KING.

BLOOM
(smiling, manic)
Let’s make this a show, shall we?

With a blinding motion, BLOOM lunges at the now-evolved EMERGENT KING, sending shockwaves through the water. The massive fish evades, barely.

CHIP
(pointing)
Oyster! The carapace! Back of the left fin—cracks when it moves!

OYSTER

The FROSTSTALKER KING and MAW KING rush BLOOM together, ice and serrated strength colliding with glowing purple flesh. The DEADWAKE QUEEN darts in from above, tail crackling with venom.

BLOOM
(snarl)
You’re all just players in a game you didn’t make.

Suddenly—BOOM! BLOOM explodes into hundreds of identical clones, all wielding violet blades, whipping them through the water in a violent storm.

CHIP
They’re illusions! Scattering signals—wait… that one! Eleven degrees northeast!

OYSTER
Say less!

OYSTER flips, aims his OXYGEN PRESSURE BLASTER, and fires a supercharged burst into the correct BLOOM. It SLAMS against the coral wall, cracking the stone. BLOOM shrieks, blood trailing.

BLOOM
(gritting teeth)
So be it.

It vanishes into a blur, zipping through the chaos, then—SLASH!—a blade PIERCES the DEADWAKE QUEEN’S side. Poison leaks from her body, filling the battlefield.

DEADWAKE QUEEN
(grinning, in pain)
You’ll need more… than that…

But BLOOM isn’t done. Behind them, the MAW KING twitches—his eyes glowing faint purple.

OYSTER
Chip—what’s wrong with the Maw King?

CHIP
No… he’s compromised. It’s controlling him!

Before they can stop him, the MAW KING turns—SLICES the DEADWAKE QUEEN in half. Her body floats, limp, her power gone.

CHIP
NO!

BLOOM uses the MAW KING like a ragdoll, and with a sickening motion, forces him into its own mouth. Crunch. Gone.

Silence. Then—BOOM. OYSTER and CHIP are flung back.

OYSTER
(panting, bleeding)
It’s just us now…

CHIP
Then let’s finish this together.

OYSTER climbs onto the FROSTSTALKER KING’s back, gripping tightly. CHIP transforms into his blaster form.

FROSTSTALKER KING
Let us make history.

With a roar, the KING rockets forward, Oyster on his back, Chip mounted like a cannon. They fire a direct blast into BLOOM, shooting it directly into the EMERGENT KING’S mouth. The king bites down—

BOOM.
BLOOM self-destructs.

The EMERGENT KING’S body implodes in a mass of gore and violet light. Everything slows. From the glowing water emerge ghostly, translucent images of the DEADWAKE QUEEN, MAW KING, EMERGENT KING, and the BLOOM himself.

DEADWAKE QUEEN (spirit)
You held your ground, little ones.

MAW KING (spirit)
And defied the monster I once was.

EMERGENT KING (spirit)
The ocean owes you peace.

BLOOM (spirit)
And now… you are what remains of the five.

The spirits fade. The FROSTSTALKER KING turns, bloodied and bruised.

FROSTSTALKER KING
(somber)
My kingdom… is all that’s left. We are the top of the food chain now.

He walks toward the darkness of the ocean, his army gathering behind him.

FROSTSTALKER KING
(looking back at Oyster)
Take care of the light, surface-dwellers.

He vanishes.

In the still water, the BLOOM’S final flicker of light pulses, then fades…

BLOOM (voice, soft)
You’ve come far. Go to the castle now.
End this.
You’ve earned your peace.

The light disappears.

OYSTER and CHIP look ahead, the ocean parting to reveal a distant, glowing CASTLE silhouetted in the abyss.

CHIP
Ready?

OYSTER
Let’s finish this.

They swim forward. Toward the final truth.

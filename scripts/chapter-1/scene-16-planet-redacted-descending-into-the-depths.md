EXT. PLANET (REDACTED) – DESCENDING INTO THE DEPTHS

They swim downward. The familiar glow of the kingdom fades behind them, replaced by the cold weight of deeper waters. Darkness thickens around them. Oyster turns on his helmet lights.

CHIP
So… any idea where we’re headed? Or are we just following your gut again?

OYSTER
If my gut’s kept us alive this long, I’m sticking with it.

A shimmer—faint at first—begins to pulse in the distance. A purple glow, delicate as silk, begins to thread through the water below like an underwater aurora.

CHIP
Wait… you seeing that?

OYSTER
Yeah. I think it’s… showing us the way.

CHIP
Or luring us off it.

OYSTER
We won’t know until we get closer.

They keep descending, the purple light growing stronger, more enticing. It dances across their suits, reflecting like stars in their helmets. But it doesn’t feel warm. It feels… curious. Alive.

And they have no idea they’ve already taken the wrong turn.

FADE TO BLACK.

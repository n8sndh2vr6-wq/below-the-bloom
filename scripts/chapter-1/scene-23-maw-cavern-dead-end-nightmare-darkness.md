INT. MAW CAVERN – DEAD END – NIGHTMARE DARKNESS

OYSTER backs into a jagged, glowing purple wall. His right hand is gone, a cloth tied tightly around the wrist. The MAW KING prowls forward, eyes gleaming, teeth bared. Dozens of other MAWS crowd behind him, watching in silence.

MAW KING
(soft, cruel)
So young. So full of fight. But your kind always folds. Always breaks.

OYSTER’s chest heaves. Blood drips. His back hits the wall. Nowhere to go.

OYSTER
(spits)
Then finish it already, freak.

MAW KING
Oh no… I savor the end.

Suddenly—CLINK. A metallic object bounces off the wall and lands in the dirt at Oyster’s feet. It’s his oxygen pressure blaster.

CHIP (O.S.)
(yelling)
NOW! SHOOT! Shoot his damn face!

The MAW KING immediately bolts forward, his mouth stretching wide like a serpent’s.

OYSTER snatches the blaster with his one good hand and fires.

FWOOOOOSH!

A concentrated stream of compressed oxygen SLAMS into the Maw King’s mouth. The blast pierces clean through his cheek with a sickening, wet hiss.

MAW KING
(snarling, enraged scream)
RRRRAAAAAAHHHH!!

He stumbles back, clutching his face, screeching. The surrounding MAWS hiss and retreat, fading into the dark one by one, leaving only the echo of their howls.

Silence.

A faint whirr above. A small light floats down from the shadows of the ceiling, revealing—CHIP.

CHIP
(hovering down)
Miss me, one-hand?

OYSTER collapses to his knees, panting, grinning in disbelief.

OYSTER
You… holy crap. You found me.

CHIP
Course I did. You think I’d let you die alone? Idiot.

OYSTER
(looking down at the blaster)

CHIP
That was the first thing I found. Then I followed the screaming. Thought, “yep, that’s my guy.”

OYSTER lets out a short laugh, shaking slightly.

OYSTER
They were gonna kill me, Chip. I—I thought I was done.

CHIP
Nah. You’re not done. You’re just getting started.

OYSTER
(quiet)
I missed you, man.

CHIP
Same here. Now get up, you dramatic meatbag. We’ve got a kingdom to burn through.

Oyster nods, using the blaster to pull himself to his feet. Behind them, the cave still glows purple. But the Maw King is gone—for now.

OYSTER
Let’s move.

They disappear into the dark, together once more.

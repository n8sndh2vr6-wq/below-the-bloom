INT. DARK MAW TERRITORY – JUST AFTER THE REUNION

The water is still for a moment. Oyster floats, catching his breath, while Chip hovers nearby—lights blinking softly. The tension lingers, but it’s eased by a moment of wonder.

CHIP
(still hovering, cheekily)
So… you’re welcome for the save, by the way. No big deal—just diving headfirst into a hoard of angry ghouls to drop you a weapon mid-death scream.

OYSTER
(smiling weakly)
Yeah, yeah. You’re a hero. Want a medal or a polish?

CHIP
Actually, I want you to shut up and look what I found.

Chip buzzes and flips, shifting his shape as he transforms mid-air. A mechanical whir hums through the water as parts rotate and click. He opens like a transformer—revealing a small embedded toolbox inside.

OYSTER
(stunned)
What the hell?

CHIP
I found a toolbox hard drive jammed in one of the crashed sub-pods near the drop zone. Integrated it into my system while I was dodging sonar fangs and existential dread.

From the center compartment, a sleek, silvery metal hand slides out, softly glowing with circuitry. It matches the shape of Oyster’s severed wrist.

CHIP
Ta-da. Prosthetic hand—pressure-sealed, neural-link capable, and it even has a bottle opener.

OYSTER
You’re insane.

CHIP
Takes one to argue with one. Now shut up and let’s get you geared.

Oyster takes the hand and fits it carefully onto his severed wrist. It magnetizes and syncs instantly with a hiss and a pulse. His eyes widen as he flexes the new hand—each finger responding like it’s always been there.

OYSTER
Whoa…

CHIP
Welcome back, Two-Handed Terror.

They both pause. Silence surrounds them.

CHIP
Ready to finish this?

OYSTER
(quietly)
Let’s find that castle.

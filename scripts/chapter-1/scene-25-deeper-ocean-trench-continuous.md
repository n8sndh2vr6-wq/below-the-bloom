EXT. DEEPER OCEAN TRENCH – CONTINUOUS

The two descend again. The water darkens further. The light from above is gone. Only Chip’s projection and Oyster’s mask lights guide the way.

*Suddenly—a PURPLE GLOW pulses ahead.

CHIP
There it is again… same glow that hit the rocket.

OYSTER
No way back now.

They follow it. The glow slithers through the black like it’s calling them forward. They come upon a narrow crevice between jagged walls—barely wide enough to swim through one at a time.

CHIP
This feels like a trap.

OYSTER
Everything’s a trap.

They plunge in. The tunnel squeezes them, forcing Chip to compress and move in short bursts, hovering just ahead. Their lights flicker as rocks scrape against the suit.

Then—suddenly—

They emerge.

The narrow passage gives way to a massive, endless chasm, flooded with open ocean. Strange coral pulses at the edges. The purple glow disappears into the distance…

CHIP
Whoa.

OYSTER
Yeah… this is it. Next chapter.

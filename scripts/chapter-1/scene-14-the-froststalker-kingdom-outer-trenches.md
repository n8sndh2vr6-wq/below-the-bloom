SCENE: The Froststalker Kingdom - Outer Trenches

The glow of bio-luminescent crystals lines the cavern walls as Oyster and Chip exit the cave. The trenches stretch before them—twisting ice bridges, glowing vents, and froststalkers moving through their territory in organized patterns. Peaceful. Structured. Surprisingly alive.

OYSTER (stepping cautiously across the icy floor)
“…This is not what I expected. Not even close.”

CHIP (projecting softly beside him)
“Did you expect them to be painting murals and hauling scrap metal like engineers?”

OYSTER (half-laughing)
“No, I expected to be torn in half five minutes after landing here.”

A small group of froststalkers carries pieces of the crashed ship across a frozen path above. Others tend to nests built into icy alcoves.

CHIP
“It’s almost… civil. Like they’ve been building this for a long time. A society hidden in the deep.”

OYSTER (quietly)
“Yeah. And we crashed straight into the middle of it. I wonder how many things we’ve misjudged like this.”

CHIP
“Probably all of them. Especially me.”

OYSTER (grinning)
“Especially you.”

They both chuckle under their breath. The quiet hum of the trench surrounds them, soothing after so much chaos.

CHIP
“Hard to believe you stabbed one of these guys in the neck twenty-four hours ago.”

OYSTER
“That one was trying to eat me.”

CHIP
“Fair point.”

A young froststalker cub scurries past with a chunk of metal, struggling to carry it. An adult nearby gently nudges it along, growling softly in encouragement.

OYSTER (watching them go)
“Maybe they’re not monsters after all.”

CHIP
“Or maybe everyone’s just a monster in someone else’s story.”

OYSTER (turning to Chip)
“…That’s deep for a floating projector.”

CHIP
“I downloaded a philosophy pack last week. You were too busy stabbing things to notice.”

OYSTER (laughing again)
“Let’s just hope the rest of the planet is as understanding as they’ve been.”

CHIP
“Yeah… we’ve still got a long swim to that castle.”

OYSTER (gaze drifting upward to the icy ceiling)
“Then we better get moving.”

The two press forward, deeper into the Froststalker kingdom, the glow of the icy trenches reflecting in Oyster’s blue eyes.

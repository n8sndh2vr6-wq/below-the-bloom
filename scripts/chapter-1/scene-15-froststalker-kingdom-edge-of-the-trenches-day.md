INT. FROSTSTALKER KINGDOM - EDGE OF THE TRENCHES – DAY

Oyster and Chip stand with the Froststalker King one last time. The ice around them glows with the soft shimmer of frost and bioluminescence. The air (or rather, the water) is quiet—peaceful. Oyster adjusts the straps on his suit, eyes serious but respectful.

OYSTER
We’re grateful for what you’ve done. For trusting us. But we have to keep going. There’s something deeper… something we have to find.

The King lowers his head, massive and solemn.

FROSTSTALKER KING
The deeper you go, the less you’ll find of yourselves. Still… I see the determination in you. May your journey not cost you more than you’re willing to give.

Chip floats beside Oyster, softly lighting up the mask.

CHIP
That’s the most dramatic way anyone’s ever said “good luck.”

The King lets out a slow exhale—almost a laugh—and then swims back toward the heart of the kingdom. Oyster watches him disappear into the glow of the trenches.

OYSTER
(clearly moved)
He’s not what I expected.

CHIP
None of them are.

INT. DEEP OCEAN – NEW BIOME – DAY/NIGHT CYCLE BLURRED

The ocean is darker here, lit by pulsing bioluminescence scattered like stars. Soft violet waves from above shimmer faintly down. Oyster’s breathing is slower now, more methodical. Chip hovers nearby, light scanning in subtle arcs.

OYSTER
(stomach growling)
I’d kill for a sandwich.

CHIP
Correction: statistically, you’re more likely to kill a fish right now. Sandwiches are out of stock.

OYSTER
(grinning, tired)
You’re hilarious. Let’s find something edible before I start chewing on your casing.

CHIP
Cannibalism and desperation—excellent signs of mental stability.

They swim slowly through the twilight zone of the reef-like trench. Odd aquatic life wriggles and pulses nearby. Oyster raises his scanner, looking for signs of food. A few larger, finned creatures—like glowing alien tuna—dart ahead.

OYSTER
(spotting a group)
There. I’ll try not to miss.

He unslings the knife from his side, moving with quick, deliberate precision. In one clean swoop, he slices through the water and skewers a fish. It flails and then goes limp. Another gets caught in a net Chip unfolds from his chassis.

CHIP
I rate your hunting technique a solid 6.3 out of 10. Room for flair.

OYSTER
(flashing a smile)
I had flair. Then I lost it somewhere around getting my hand sliced off.

They pause near a glowing coral patch. Oyster begins carefully cooking the fish on a heated panel from his suit’s utility module.

CHIP
So. While we dine on alien sashimi—what was life like for you… before?

OYSTER
(pensive, soft)
I had parents. A house near the coast. I built kites out of junk parts. Had dreams about flying—not diving. Funny, huh?

CHIP
Flying’s just falling in reverse. You were halfway there.

They both sit—Oyster floating, Chip hovering. The fish sizzles faintly. It smells vaguely citrusy, oddly comforting.

OYSTER
What about you? Any… past life as a coffee maker?

CHIP
Negative. I was born in a toolbox and promoted to best friend.

Suddenly, the water around them dims. A faint purple light pulses to life. It begins swirling around one of the fish—still swimming nearby, untouched. The fish begins spinning in a strange pattern, slowly descending into the trench below.

OYSTER
(confused)
That light again. It’s… circling it.

CHIP
That’s not bioluminescence. That’s direction. Intent.

OYSTER
Come on. Let’s follow it.

They swim cautiously, following the fish and the dancing purple glow as it dives deeper, leaving the comfort of the glowing coral behind. The light guides them like a lure, vanishing slowly into the darker waters below.

FADE TO BLACK.

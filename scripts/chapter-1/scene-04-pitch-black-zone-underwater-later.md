INT. PITCH BLACK ZONE – UNDERWATER – LATER

Oyster and Chip descend deeper into the abyss. The shallows above them shrink to a distant shimmer of light. All around is silence, black water stretching into endlessness.

OYSTER
(quietly)
This is it… the pitch zone.

Chip floats close, lights dimmed. The darkness thickens, wrapping around them like ink.

CHIP
Oxygen levels stable. Visibility: zero.
(beat)
Activating mask lights.

CLICK. Oyster’s tech mask flares to life, casting soft blue beams forward. The surroundings glow faintly in the silt-heavy water—an eerie underwater forest of rock, bone, and decay.

There’s a long silence. Then—

WHUMP.
From the shadows, a huge figure rushes toward them.

OYSTER
Whoa—MOVE!

A FROSTSTALKER—scarred, towering, twisted with battle-worn fins—lunges forward, jaws open, spikes aimed like a battering ram. Oyster ducks behind a jagged rock as Chip zips into a crevice.

CHIP
That’s not just any froststalker—that’s a berserker class.

OYSTER
It didn’t even wait to sniff us!

The creature circles, searching, its breath gurgling through rows of bloodstained teeth. Then—its head jerks.

CHIP (V.O.)
It heard us.

ROOOAAARRR. The Froststalker crashes through the rock, sending splinters of stone flying. Oyster rolls to the side, drawing his oxygen blaster.

OYSTER
Alright, screw hiding!

He aims, fires—FWOOOOSH!—a high-pressure blast tears into the creature’s flank. It shrieks and spins, tail whipping violently. Chip projects a distraction hologram behind it.

CHIP
Target its underjaw. It’s the weakest armor point.

Oyster dashes forward as the creature thrashes. He vaults onto its back, stabbing his knife into the spine just below the neck. The beast bucks and rages, shaking him violently.

OYSTER
C’mon… c’mon!

The Froststalker flings him into the sand. As it pounces again, Chip fires a flash from his projector, blinding it momentarily. Oyster leaps, thrusts his blade upward—deep into its throat.

The beast shudders. Still. Water clouds with dark blood. Its body slowly sinks to the seabed.

CHIP
Confirmed neutralized.
(beat)
You okay?

OYSTER
(grimly)
Yeah… yeah.
(looking down at it)
That’s not gonna be the last one, is it?

CHIP
Nope. Not even close.

OYSTER
Then let’s keep moving.

They continue into the black, the silence of the ocean returning as the froststalker fades behind them.

Car-sized, insect-winged, white-furred, and hunting in swarms out of the sand
that buried the [Emergent Kingdom](#/locations/emergent-kingdom). Oyster's first
lesson about them is also the Agency's: *supposed to be mythical.*

> **CHIP:** Piercers! I thought they were extinct!
>
> **OYSTER:** They were supposed to be mythical. First agent training — escape
> vertically!

## Anatomy

Beaks that flash like blades and chest cages that open to expose glowing organs
and edges — the same cavity a [Piercer Queen](#/characters/piercer-queen) later
uses to threaten, and the same one Oyster climbs inside to end her.

They hunt in coordinated swarms, but the coordination comes from her. Cut the
head off — literally, in [Scene 27](#/story/chapter-2/scene-27) — and the swarm
scatters.

## Pic

Not every Piercer that hatches is a weapon. One, small and matted-furred, is
found twitching in the wreckage of the ambush that opens
[Scene 7](#/story/chapter-2/scene-07), too weak to return to the sand and too
young to have learned to be afraid of people yet. Chip feeds it. It follows them
home.

See [Pic](#/characters/pic).

## The Queen's ultimatum

Piercers are sapient enough to negotiate through their ruler, and their ruler is
absolute. When she finds her missing child among the heroes she does not ask
twice.

> **PIERCER QUEEN:** My child is amongst you. Return him… or suffer the
> consequences.

## Where they turn up

Not just the Emergent ruins. A second swarm bursts up from beneath the sand of
an Acidic dead end in [Scene 24](#/story/chapter-2/scene-24) — proof they nest
wherever the ocean floor is soft enough, including underneath the
[icy trenches](#/locations/icy-trenches) — and Pic vouches for the heroes just in
time to turn them from a second ambush into a rescue.

> **OYSTER:** Show them! Tell them you're one of them!

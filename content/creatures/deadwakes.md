Long, eel-like, armoured, and streaked with bioluminescence. They hold the
[purple paradise](#/locations/purple-paradise) — the most beautiful water in the
chapter — and they are the reason it is empty.

## In a fight

A Deadwake screeches like ripping metal and moves with *unnatural grace*. It
does not simply attack; it reads you.

> **CHIP:** It's reading your moves! Try something stupid!

Oyster fires the blaster and it dodges effortlessly. He lunges with the knife and
misses. It only loses because Chip rams it at full speed and breaks its hold,
and because Oyster then puts the blaster into the back of its throat.

## The poison

Killing one is worse than losing to one. Deadwake blood is a purple poison that
does not disperse into the water — it **calls**.

> **CHIP:** Oh no. Oh no no no. That's not just blood—
>
> **OYSTER:** Poison?
>
> **CHIP:** Worse. It's calling them.

Five. Ten. A dozen more, circling, closing in. It is the only creature in the
chapter whose corpse is more dangerous than the animal.

## Their kingdom

Reached through a shadowed trench at the edge of the paradise: a cathedral-like
expanse with bioluminescent bones set into the walls, and a throne of chiselled
obsidian coral. Beautiful and horrifying, in that order.

Their [queen](#/characters/deadwake-queen) does not eat prisoners either. She
negotiates with them, which turns out to be worse.

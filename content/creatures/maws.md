The ambush predators of the [cavernous depths](#/locations/maw-territory), and
the reason every other kingdom on the planet has an army.

## What you see first

Two dim orange circles opening in total blackness. Large. Watching. Unblinking.

Then a shape blurs in from the side and a guttural screech splits the water.

## Anatomy and hunting

Jagged teeth, mandibles that flex, claws that click audibly on coral, and mouths
that stretch open like a serpent's. They hunt in the dark by preference and they
hunt in numbers — dozens will crowd a tunnel in silence to watch their king
finish something.

Their most dangerous trait is not physical. Maw territory generates
**electromagnetic spikes** that jam electronics, and it is a spike — not a bite
— that separates Oyster from [Chip](#/characters/chip) for four scenes in
[Scene 17](#/story/chapter-1/scene-17).

## Why they matter

The Maws are the fixed point the rest of the ocean organises against. The
[Froststalkers](#/creatures/froststalkers) and [Emergents](#/creatures/emergents)
ended a war between themselves specifically so they could survive the
[annual Maw hunger](#/lore/the-annual-war) together.

They also range further than their own territory. The dead Maw that earns Oyster
his freedom in [Scene 12](#/story/chapter-1/scene-12) is one that got into a
Froststalker nursery.

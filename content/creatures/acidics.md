Tall, black, bony, and slicked with dark mucus. No eyes. No mouth — until it
peels open like a demonic flower and the hands unroll into burning tendrils that
melt rock on contact.

> Its hands slowly unroll into grotesque burning tendrils, oozing and hissing as
> they meet the cave walls. The rock begins to melt and steam.

Nobody in [Scene 15](#/story/chapter-2/scene-15) sees one coming, and the
Froststalker caves are only the first place they show up. By
[Scene 16](#/story/chapter-2/scene-16) the entire [icy trenches](#/locations/icy-trenches)
are a wasteland, and Acidics are pouring in from every direction alongside a
Piercer swarm.

## Fighting them

They are faster and more numerous than anything the heroes have fought before,
which is why the plan is never to win — only to stall.

> **OYSTER:** We don't kill. We stall. Hold 'em.

Killing one still works: a katana through the ribs, a blast at point-blank
range, a wall knocked down on top of one mid-charge. What doesn't work is
standing still. Their acid eats through pressure suits, coats a bubble around a
suffocating Oyster, and shorts out Chip's systems outright in
[Scene 31](#/story/chapter-2/scene-31).

## Their king

See [the Acidic King](#/characters/acidic-king) — bigger, six-legged, and
entirely willing to fight fair and still lose.

## What isn't known

Where they come from is never said. They simply arrive, in force, the same
night Squid's "ancient powers" become worth chasing again — and they are the
only one of the chapter's threats with no home shown, no kingdom described, and
no history anyone in the story claims to know.

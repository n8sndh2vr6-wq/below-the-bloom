Nobody in Chapter Two sees a whole Skywalker. What they see is legs — spider-crab
legs as thick as towers, dozens of them, hammering down through a cavern in a
slow earth-shaking march and crushing anything already dead or dying underfoot.

> Massive spider-crab legs — Skywalker legs — hammer the cavern floor in a
> rhythmic, earth-shaking march. Dozens of them, each as thick as a tower, blot
> out the soft glow above.

## What they do, not what they are

They arrive right after the [Acidics](#/creatures/acidics) are beaten back in
[Scene 30](#/story/chapter-2/scene-30), close a corralling circle around the
group without any apparent malice, and nearly finish what the siege couldn't.

> **MAW KING:** They're corralling us!

Oyster splits the ring with a katana strike to a single leg; the group breaks
free through the gap before it can reseal, and the legs stomp on past into the
dark, uninterested, toward wherever the rest of the thing is.

## Why they matter

They are the closest the chapter comes to acknowledging that the ocean has
layers deeper than anything named — [the Emergent King](#/characters/emergent-king)
called them the oldest things alive, and something the size of a Skywalker was
never in that count.

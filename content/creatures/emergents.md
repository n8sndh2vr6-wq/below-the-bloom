The oldest beings on the planet, and the most dangerous thing in the ocean to
underestimate — which is exactly what Oyster does, because he is hungry.

## The disguise

An Emergent can hold the shape of a small glowing fish indefinitely. It looks
like food. It behaves like food. It will lead you somewhere if you follow it.

Then something disturbs it, and it changes.

> Its body contorting. Limbs extend. Plates form. Scales harden. It stretches and
> grows at a terrifying rate, morphing into a massive, ancient sea beast — like a
> fusion of a shark and a dragon, its body the size of the iceberg that sank the
> Titanic.

Its mouth opens and the water surges toward it like a black hole. Oyster escapes
in [Scene 27](#/story/chapter-1/scene-27) only by firing his oxygen blaster
*downward* and using the recoil.

> **CHIP:** Oh no. Oh no no no — OYSTER YOU JUST STABBED A BABY EMERGENT.

## Abilities

Beyond the transformation, the Emergent King is seen summoning whirlpools of
pressure during the war in [Scene 32](#/story/chapter-1/scene-32). Whatever they
are, they are not simply large animals.

## Their society

Their kingdom sits in the [lighted area](#/locations/emergent-kingdom) below Maw
country — the first place in the chapter where natural light comes from
*beneath* you. Structures of coral, ice and salvaged metal rise like a cityscape.
Visiting [Froststalkers](#/creatures/froststalkers) swim its plazas peacefully.

> **OYSTER:** Well, if that thing's the ruler of wherever we are now… we're
> officially trespassing in the gods' garden.
>
> **CHIP:** Next time maybe don't shank the garden gnome.

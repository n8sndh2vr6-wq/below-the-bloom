A terrible screech cuts through the depths. The DEADWAKE QUEEN charges. Fast. Unrelenting. She strikes down almost every ally they’ve earned. Only OYSTER, CHIP, and the three KINGS remain.

Together, in synchronized battle, they take her down. She falls. Breathing. Eyes clearing. She joins them.

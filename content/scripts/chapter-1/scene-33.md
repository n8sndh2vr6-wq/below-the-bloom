Chaos swirls like a vortex. DEADWAKES plunge from above, claws flailing, jaws snapping. CREATURES of every kind scatter or charge. It’s a full-blown ocean war.

OYSTER and CHIP swim side-by-side, blades out, pressure blaster at the ready. But then—

A MASSIVE THUMP.

Everything stops.

All eyes turn as a colossal MAW descends from above, bigger than any seen before. His body glows faintly in the purple hue, glowing scars and ceremonial armor marking his age and rank—he is the MAW KING.

He lands between the FROSTSTALKER KING, the EMERGENT KING, and the DEADWAKE QUEEN. OYSTER and CHIP drift nearby, breath held, waiting.

SILENCE. Not a ripple stirs.

MAW KING (soft, dark voice): We are not allies. Not kin. Not even neighbors. But I know who I will never let rule this ocean.

The DEADWAKE QUEEN’s eye twitches. She begins to speak—

SOUND: SLASH.

A quick pivot—faster than anything his size should be capable of. The MAW KING’s claws rake across the QUEEN’S face, tearing through her eye.

MAW KING (snapping): You. You are the rot in these waters.

The FROSTSTALKER KING and EMERGENT KING roar in unison. Battle erupts once more—

OYSTER (whipping around to Chip): NOW it’s a war.

CHIP (over the chaos): Understatement of the century!

Oyster and Chip hover silently near the icy formations, the blue glow from the ice flickering across their suits. The air is thick with tension after the herd of Froststalkers passed by.

CHIP (low voice): I think we’re clear. That was… way too close.

OYSTER (whispers): Yeah. Let’s keep moving—quietly.

Suddenly, two non-berserker Froststalkers emerge from the shadows, slinking forward with calculated steps, sniffing at the water, low growls vibrating the current. One sniffs Oyster’s arm.

CHIP: Uh… Oyster?

The first Froststalker snaps its jaw suddenly, grazing Oyster’s arm. Oyster yells in pain.

OYSTER (angrily): They’re not friendly.

CHIP: No hiding this time!

Oyster launches forward, slashing at the first Froststalker’s face with his knife. It snarls and claws back, raking across Oyster’s side. He dodges, spins underwater, and blasts it point-blank with the oxygen blaster, creating a burst of bubbles and a deep wound.

The Froststalker reels back—then Oyster charges and buries the knife into its throat. It twitches, floats limp. No time to breathe.

KILL: Oyster > Froststalker sentry | knife buried in the throat

CHIP: Behind you!

The second one lunges—Oyster grabs the dead creature’s fin, yanks it hard, and breaks it free with a grunt. The fin is jagged, edged with ice-hardened cartilage. He turns and swings it like a blade, slamming it across the second Froststalker’s face.

OYSTER (gritted teeth): Come on then.

The creature charges again—Oyster stabs it in the neck with the broken fin and fires the blaster into its open jaw. The creature screams in a low, watery rumble, then floats down, lifeless.

KILL: Oyster > Froststalker sentry | broken fin to the neck, blaster into the jaw

Oyster hovers in silence, breathing heavily.

CHIP: …You alright?

OYSTER: Just… tired of fighting.

CHIP: I’ll add “makeshift weapons specialist” to your resume.

They float silently over the two corpses, blood and bubbles drifting into the dark like falling snow.

CHIP: Let’s move before more show up.

They continue, swimming cautiously into the kingdom’s depths, past the glowing ice that now feels colder than ever.

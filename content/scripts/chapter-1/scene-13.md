Oyster and Chip re-enter the cell. Oyster picks up his oxygen blaster and scanner from the floor. The glow of their tech reflects off the icy walls.

CHIP: Still think you’re just a lucky survivor?

OYSTER (grins faintly): I think I’m a guy who just signed a weird alliance with a giant snow lizard.

CHIP: He has a crown made of teeth, you know that? Literal teeth.

OYSTER: Yeah. And I think… I think he trusted us. Not because we were strong. But because we didn’t walk away.

A quiet moment. They sit together for a second—just breathing. The glow of the blaster dims in Oyster’s lap.

CHIP: Think the agency would believe this?

OYSTER: They’ll believe it when they see the bruises.

They both chuckle. The silence that follows is warm this time.

The descent has grown quiet. All sound feels muted by the crushing depth. The purple glow has long since faded, and now, the water is a murky, eerie gray. Chip floats just above Oyster’s shoulder, casting dim light.

OYSTER (quietly): Still no bottom. We’ve been going for—what? Half an hour?

CHIP: Thirty-six minutes. At this depth, I should be rerouting power to reinforce the—

Suddenly, Chip’s hologram flickers violently.

CHIP (glitching): Warning. Electromagnetic—s-s-spike de-detected—

Fzzzt.

The light from Oyster’s mask flickers, dies. Chip’s voice cuts out entirely. Total blackness surrounds them.

OYSTER: Chip? CHIP?

Nothing. The darkness hums. Pressure presses against him like a giant’s hand.

Then—two dim orange circles appear in the void. Large. Watching. Unblinking.

OYSTER (whispering): No, no, no…

He grabs his oxygen blaster, raises it, steadying his breath.

But before he can act—A SHAPE BLURS OUT FROM THE SIDE.

A deafening, guttural screech splits the silence as something SLAMS into Oyster. He spins in the water, narrowly escaping jagged teeth.

OYSTER: CHIP! WHERE ARE YOU?

A panicked pulse of sonar pings out once from his suit.

CHIP (distant, staticky): Oys—ter—I’m—jammed! T-t-take cover!

Another shadow streaks through. Oyster and Chip are both scattered—each shoved in opposite directions by monstrous limbs.

Separated. Alone.

Oyster floats into silence. The only sound is his breath echoing in the helmet.

OYSTER (to himself): Okay. Okay, okay… Stay alive. Find Chip.

Somewhere out in the black… a whisper of a growl rumbles through the water.

The glow of the mask-lights hum softly in the shadows. Oyster sits near the edge of the cave wall, his breath slow, his body still. Chip floats quietly beside him.

SOUND: [DULL THUD]

A sound. Muffled. Then another. A screech. A high-pitched, painful screech—like metal being torn underwater.

CHIP (sharply): That’s not normal ambient trench acoustics.

OYSTER (standing quickly): That sounded like a Froststalker. A young one.

The noise behind the wall grows louder—guttural growls, yelps, the sound of slamming bodies and snarls of something alien. Oyster steps toward the wall and presses his scanner against it. It lights up red—heat signatures swirl violently.

CHIP: There’s a breach point right here. You can blast it. But it’s a risk.

OYSTER: We’re already prisoners. What’s another bad decision?

He pulls out the oxygen blaster, aims it at the wall’s weakest point, and fires. With a violent CRACK, the wall splits open, and freezing water floods in through the gap, revealing a hidden chamber.

SCENE: INT. HIDDEN CHAMBER – CONTINUOUS

The chamber is chaos. Two small baby Froststalkers—barely half the size of an adult—whimper as a hulking Maw circles them. Its jaws unhinged, blood already drifting in clouds around its jagged teeth. One baby lies injured; the other snarls, trying to defend its sibling.

CHIP: That’s a Maw. And not a curious one.

OYSTER (leveling his blaster): Back. Off.

He fires a blast of oxygen directly at the Maw’s head—it staggers but recovers quickly, slamming its tail into a nearby rock, sending shards flying. It turns toward Oyster with glowing, murderous eyes.

Oyster lunges forward, dodging the tail swing and blasting the Maw again. This time it screams—a low, vibrating howl. It snaps its jaws, narrowly missing him.

CHIP (yelling): Left flank! It’s circling!

Oyster spins, grabs a metal spike from the broken wall, and drives it into the Maw’s side. The beast thrashes, disoriented, then rushes toward the babies again.

OYSTER (furious): No you don’t.

He dives between them, pulling out his knife. The Maw clamps down, nearly catching his leg—but he thrusts the blade up under its jaw. A final oxygen blast follows, directly into the wound.

The Maw lets out a final, distorted groan and goes limp, its body floating lifeless in the chamber.

Silence returns—except for the soft whimpers of the baby Froststalkers.

CHIP: They’re… still breathing.

OYSTER (crouching down, looking at them): They’re not just monsters. They’re kids.

The two baby Froststalkers blink at him, one nuzzling close cautiously. Oyster reaches out, gently placing his hand near their snouts.

CHIP: They’re gonna remember that.

OYSTER: Good. Maybe someone should.

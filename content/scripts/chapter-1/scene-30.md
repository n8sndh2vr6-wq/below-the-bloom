The camera pans over the shimmering, alien beauty of the purple paradise. Twisting coral spires and luminous plant life sway in gentle currents. But the serenity is shattered as Oyster and Chip float, wounded and breathing hard, surrounded by dozens of circling Deadwakes.

OYSTER (back pressed against a rock, clutching his bleeding chest): There’s too many… We’re done, Chip.

CHIP (voice sharp, glitching slightly): Calculating… zero routes of escape. Recommend a tactical surrender—preferably without the dying part.

The circling Deadwakes part. A massive, towering figure glides toward them. Sleek, elegant, terrifying—the Deadwake Queen. Her bioluminescent fins pulse with dominance. Her eyes lock onto Oyster.

DEADWAKE QUEEN (her voice is smooth, regal, but sharp like a blade): You two… Intriguing. Come. You will follow.

OYSTER (groans, still holding his wound): Not really feeling like I’ve got a choice here…

CHIP: Obedience: recommended. Defiance: likely fatal.

They are flanked by two enormous guards and begin swimming toward a shadowed trench at the far edge of the paradise. The trench leads into a cathedral-like expanse—this is the Deadwake Kingdom. Bioluminescent bones line the walls. It’s beautiful, and horrifying.

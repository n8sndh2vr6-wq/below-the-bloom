The stillness is thick. Oyster and Chip float in awe, surrounded by shimmering coral, glowing plants, and misty purple light. The BOOMING VOICE continues to echo through the chamber.

THE BLOOM (V.O.): You have come far. Tell me, child of ash and code… Why did you follow the purple trail?

OYSTER (breathless): Because… it felt like it knew where we were meant to go. Like something bigger was pulling us here.

THE BLOOM (V.O.): And what was your favorite part of the journey?

CHIP (softly, almost nostalgic): I’d say the part where we thought we were safe — the Froststalker kingdom. It was… peaceful. Hopeful.

THE BLOOM (V.O.): Then why… why did you leave the safety of the frost?

OYSTER (hesitating, then honest): Because I had to know. We had to know. What was at the end of all this. We’ve lost too much not to.

A low hum fills the chamber. The purple mist begins to spiral inward as something stirs from its depths. Slowly, a sleek, glowing fish emerges. Its scales ripple with power — light and shadow shifting like thoughts. Its voice now speaks directly, mouth unmoving, eyes ancient and calm.

THE BLOOM: Then I shall show you. I am the current, the tide, the teeth, the whisper between each breath in the dark. I do not command this world. I am this world. I am The Bloom.

Oyster and Chip exchange a tense glance. Behind them, a rumbling sound — like distant thunder — begins to echo from above.

THE BLOOM (without turning): And now… the wheel must turn again.

With a single ripple of its fins, a shockwave pulses through the chamber. A SILENT FLASH. The scene changes — not in location, but in weight. The BATTLE ABOVE UNFREEZES. Creatures FALL from the suspended time into the ocean floor — limp but conscious, breathing heavy, confused.

The Froststalker King crashes down beside them, his breathing labored. He looks up — eyes wide in horror.

FROSTSTALKER KING: Do not speak to it! That thing— it twists fate itself!

The Bloom turns its gaze to him, calm and eerily soft.

THE BLOOM: Fate is not twisted. It is followed. You mistook your choices for control. There is only ever the current.

Suddenly — the limp bodies around the battlefield begin to RISE.

The Froststalkers. The Maws. The Deadwakes. The Emergents. Their bodies float, slowly rising in unison. Their EYES GLOW PURPLE.

They are awake… but not in control.

Oyster steps back, drawing his knife. Chip hovers near his shoulder, tools twitching open.

OYSTER (coldly): You planned this…

THE BLOOM: I am this. The war was never yours to win. It was the cycle. And you… you are the final breath of it.

All around them, the possessed creatures FLOAT, still, waiting.

The Bloom smiles.

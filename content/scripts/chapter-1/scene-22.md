Oyster races down the corridor. Behind him, faint echoes of roars and the scuttling of Maws fill the air.

OYSTER (voice breaking): Chip… where are you?!

He rounds a corner — only to find a dead end: a smooth stone wall slick with moisture. He slams against it, turning in panic.

From the darkness behind, dozens of Maws spill into view, their orange eyes flickering. They advance silently.

OYSTER: No… no… please—

He backs against the wall, breathing ragged. The glow behind him intensifies as the Maw King steps forward from the pack, blade in hand, still smiling.

MAW KING: You cannot escape the dark, human.

Oyster glances over his shoulder at the swarm closing in. His one good hand rests on his knife’s hilt—only seconds remain.

Purple bioluminescence casts long shadows over the jagged coral floor. OYSTER kneels before the MAW KING, one hand still bound. The King unsheathes a curved blade, its edge glinting orange in the cavern light.

MAW KING (softly, savoring the moment): You would risk all to defy me, human?

He raises the blade, then slows—letting the tension stretch. Oyster’s eyes flare with pain and fury as the King brings the blade to Oyster’s bound wrist.

With deliberate slowness, the King slices through Oyster’s suit—and bone. Oyster’s scream echoes, distorted through his helmet’s speakers. Blood clouds the water.

OYSTER (through grinding teeth): You… monster…

MAW KING (leaning close): Yes. And you remain… remarkably entertaining.

The King steps back, holding the severed hand by the wrist. He watches Oyster writhe in agony.

Oyster clenches his remaining fist, eyes narrowing. The King turns away, amused, addressing his guards.

MAW KING: Finish him.

Two Maws lunge. Oyster, fueled by rage and adrenaline, suddenly springs up. He drop‑kicks the first Maw in the jaw—its mandibles snap shut on empty air as it reels back, unconscious.

Before the second Maw can react, Oyster grabs his fallen suit knife and slashes across its head. The Maw goes limp instantly, sinking to the floor.

KILL: Oyster > Maw guard | suit knife across the head

The Maw King throws head back and laughs, a cold, hollow sound that echoes through the cavern. He doesn’t lift a claw, merely watches as Oyster breathes heavily, blood staining his suit.

MAW KING (applauding slowly): Splendid. Run, then—if you can.

Oyster staggers upright and bolts past the unconscious guards. He sprints through the throne room’s archway into a narrow hallway lit by wavering purple glow.

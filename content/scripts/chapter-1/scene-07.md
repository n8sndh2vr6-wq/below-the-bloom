The water grows colder. Jagged cliffs of glacial rock rise up on all sides, casting eerie silhouettes. Oyster and Chip swim slowly, hugging the walls of the trench as schools of Froststalkers patrol the area in tight, aggressive formations.

CHIP (quietly): Keep low. There’s at least a dozen of them. They’re patrolling like soldiers.

OYSTER: Whispering doesn’t help underwater, Chip. But yeah… I see ‘em. No sudden moves.

They duck behind a jagged ice outcrop. Two Froststalkers pass directly overhead, trailing long icy fins and blades glinting in the gloom. One pauses, sniffing the water, before moving on.

CHIP: They’re moving metal. Look—pieces of our ship. They’re carrying them like… building materials.

OYSTER: They’re building something. Or reinforcing. This isn’t just a pack of beasts. This is a civilization.

They swim deeper, weaving between stone columns and glowing flora. A narrow tunnel leads into a concealed cave mouth tucked between two ridges. They slip inside, breathing heavy from the close call.

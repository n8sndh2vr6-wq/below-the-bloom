The camera lingers on the cave wall as silence settles over the room. Then—

ROARS. Deep, thunderous, echoing. Several. Drawing closer.

Oyster tenses. Chip glows brighter, aiming his small projectors down the tunnel. The cries of agony are no longer present—just these terrible, ancient howls.

CHIP (tight whisper): Uh… Oyster, I think we woke something up.

Heavy steps echo as the Froststalker King bursts through the tunnel. His massive, armored body glistens with icy mist. His luminous scars pulse a faint blue.

KING FROSTSTALKER (roaring, then stopping): My children!

He rushes forward—only to freeze. The dead Maw lies behind Oyster and Chip. The two baby Froststalkers are curled beside them, safe and breathing softly.

The King’s eyes, once wild, soften. He exhales with something between a gasp and a growl.

KING FROSTSTALKER: You… you saved them.

Oyster slowly steps back, holding up his hands.

OYSTER: We weren’t trying to escape. We just heard the noise, and… we couldn’t let them die.

CHIP: He skewered a Maw with a high-pressure oxygen stream. You should’ve seen it. Messy. Effective.

The King moves forward, his heavy claw gently nudging one of the babies. It nuzzles back. His voice drops into a deep, resonant calm.

KING FROSTSTALKER: Kindness from above… not for conquest. But to protect. That is rare. You are no longer prisoners here.

He steps aside, bowing his great head slightly.

KING FROSTSTALKER: Welcome to Frostwalker territory, Oyster and Chip. You walk now with us.

Oyster looks stunned. Chip flashes a soft beam of light upward—a quiet expression of gratitude.

The King leaves the chamber slowly, his babies padding behind him.

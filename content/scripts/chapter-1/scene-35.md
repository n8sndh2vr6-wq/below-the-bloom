As Oyster slices through the final of a triad of DEADWAKES, suddenly—

SOUND: SILENCE.

Time stops. Bubbles freeze. Creatures locked in mid-motion.

OYSTER (panicking): What the hell—Chip? Chip!?

CHIP (voice trembling): I—I don’t know. My systems are stalling. Something’s overriding… everything.

In front of them, a translucent PURPLE BUBBLE expands outward. It’s not made of water. It’s something… else. Magic, energy, alien force—unknowable.

Everyone, even the kings, are frozen inside it—locked in time. Only OYSTER and CHIP remain unaffected.

CHIP (scanning wildly): The light… Oyster, look…

A PURPLE TRAIL. Just like before. Only this time, it pulses slowly… inviting them.

Oyster stares into the shifting currents of the trail, a soft hum echoing like a whisper.

OYSTER (softly): It’s leading us again. But… this time—it wants us to follow.

They drift toward the trail, past frozen war, blood still hanging in the water like ink in zero gravity.

And down they go. Into the unknown.

OYSTER’s eyes flutter open. His mask lights sputter, casting a dim purple glow on the cavern walls. At first, he thinks he’s back in the Froststalker kingdom—until he realizes the hue is entirely different: everything here pulses in shades of violet and indigo.

OYSTER (quiet, to himself): What… where am I?

He sits up, scanning the area. The architecture is reminiscent of the icy trenches—jagged pillars, carved alcoves—but the stone and frost are replaced by smooth, organic walls that glow from within. The air (water) feels thicker, charged.

He stands, cautiously moving forward. In the distance, dozens of Maws slither along the walls and ceiling, their orange eyes flickering like beacons in the purple gloom.

OYSTER (under his breath): That glow… it’s all purple. This must be the Maw kingdom.

He presses himself flat against a wall as a pair of Maws glide past, noses twitching, scanning for movement. Oyster holds his breath, edging backwards, then creeps sideways behind a massive column.

As he peers around, he spots a faint trail of discarded Maw lures—shards of orange bioluminescent tissue—leading deeper into the cavern.

OYSTER (softly): They build here too… weird.

He follows the shards at a safe distance. More Maws appear, investigating sounds. Oyster ducks into a narrow crevice, heart pounding. His mask flickers; a low hum echoes.

Suddenly, two Maws break from the shadows ahead. Their clicking intensifies as they catch Oyster’s calm breathing.

MAW 1 (clicking, voice synthesized through Chip’s old translations): Intruder detected.

MAW 2 (clicking): Bring to King.

Before Oyster can react, they snap their jaws and lurch toward him. He bolts—swimming clumsily through the purple-lit corridor. The Maws give chase, herding him into a wider chamber.

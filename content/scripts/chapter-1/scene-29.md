SCENE: INT. DEEP OCEAN CAVERN – PURPLE PARADISE – CONTINUOUS

Soft light pours through tall, twisting coral structures glowing a serene lavender. Schools of bioluminescent fish drift through strange trees made of translucent mineral. The water is calm here, warm even. OYSTER and CHIP float in silence for a moment, overwhelmed by the beauty.

OYSTER (quietly): This place… it’s nothing like the rest of the depths.

CHIP: No screeching, no glowing eyes. Just… peace. (beat) I almost forgot what that felt like.

OYSTER: Me too. (he looks around) I mean—look at this. From that crash… to now. How are we still alive?

CHIP: Because we’re very stupid and very lucky. Mostly stupid.

OYSTER chuckles.

OYSTER: Nah. We’ve survived more than we should’ve. You know, when I was a kid, I used to think the ocean was just waves and salt. Nothing like this.

CHIP: And now you’re practically royalty in an underwater war zone. Dreams do come true.

They both float there a while longer, taking in the gentle sway of the paradise. OYSTER pulls out his knife and prepares to carve up a small glowing fish for food.

OYSTER: Thanks, little guy. I hope you’re not some sacred god or something—

SOUND: SLICE.

The moment he cuts the fish, the water shifts. The light dims slightly, and a massive shape swims by behind them with an unnatural grace. It circles once, silent. Then it strikes.

CHIP: MOVE!

A DEADWAKE slams into them, sending them tumbling. Its long, eel-like body is armored and pulsing with bioluminescent streaks. It screeches—a high, discordant sound like ripping metal. It SLASHES OYSTER across the chest with a long talon.

OYSTER (grunts): Gah—!

OYSTER clutches his chest, blood diffusing into the water. The Deadwake circles for another pass. OYSTER lifts his oxygen pressure blaster and FIRES—

SOUND: WHOOSH!

The Deadwake DODGES effortlessly.

CHIP: It’s reading your moves! Try something stupid!

OYSTER: Stupid? I’m full of that!

He lunges with his knife—MISS. The creature coils around him, but CHIP swoops down, slamming into it at full speed, disrupting its hold.

CHIP: Hey sushi-face! You forgot the AI!

Together they engage the beast—OYSTER ducking and weaving, slashing, CHIP distracting and slamming. Finally, OYSTER maneuvers behind the creature, rams the blaster into the back of its throat, and FIRES.

The DEADWAKE convulses, lets out a horrific SCREECH, and goes still. But then—

KILL: Oyster > Deadwake | blaster rammed into the back of its throat

PURPLE POISON begins to leak from its body into the water.

CHIP: Oh no. Oh no no no. That’s not just blood—

OYSTER: Poison?

CHIP: Worse. It’s calling them.

Shapes appear in the distance. Five… ten… a dozen more DEADWAKE circling them, slowly closing in.

OYSTER: We’ve got to move. Now.

The scene ends as they spin in the water, surrounded, wounded, breathing heavy—and ready.

The chamber glows brighter with each moment. The BLOOM swims slow, calm, with a presence larger than its form. Oyster and Chip hover, silent, watching. A deep hum pulses faintly, almost like a heartbeat.

THE BLOOM: This world you crash-landed on… it is not chaos. It is not war. It is balance. A food chain, perfectly tuned across time. But balance… must be maintained. The Emergent rises. The Maw consumes. The Frost stalks. The Deadwake waits. And I… I decide.

The fish circles them now, its glow brushing the rock walls in soft purple strokes. It speaks like it’s reciting a poem it’s told a thousand times.

THE BLOOM (CONT’D): Do you know what power is? Power is not control. It is belief. When all eyes turn to you for answers, when the ocean holds its breath at your presence… That is power. And I wield it for them all.

OYSTER (staring the Bloom down): Then why the war? Why pit them against each other?

THE BLOOM: Because evolution does not pause for comfort. Because something greater is coming. And because death… is the only thing that makes life matter.

Just then — a distant rumble above. The Bloom’s fins twitch. It glances up.

THE BLOOM (CONT’D): Time resumes.

A WAVE OF PURPLE energy flows upward through the rock and into the ocean above. A moment later — SPLASHES, GROANS, THE SOUND OF BODIES COLLAPSING INTO WATER.

Oyster and Chip glance upward, alarmed.

THE BLOOM (CONT’D) (still calm): They live. But none have strength left to fight.

A faint crackle — a tunnel opens. The FROSTSTALKER KING, battered, enters from above, dragging a spear behind him. His voice is filled with warning.

FROSTSTALKER KING: Do not listen to it! It bends the tide to its will — it poisons fate!

The Bloom turns, slowly.

THE BLOOM: Fate… cannot be poisoned. It is what it is. All rivers lead to me. Even yours.

The Froststalker King takes a step forward, but stumbles. The Bloom smiles gently.

THE BLOOM (CONT’D): You are all here… because I allowed it.

Chip and Oyster prepare themselves. Oyster tightens his grip on his blaster. Chip’s frame stiffens into defense mode.

CHIP: I don’t like the way it’s smiling, Oyster.

OYSTER: Yeah. Me neither.

The Bloom’s glow intensifies. Behind Oyster and Chip, the limp bodies of the three kings — Froststalker, Emergent, Maw — begin to twitch. Then rise. Purple flickers in their eyes. The same spreads to the warriors, the beasts, the dead.

All of them rise… silent. Controlled.

THE BLOOM (softly): You see? All tides return to me.

The camera lingers on Oyster and Chip — alone, surrounded by the possessed army of the ocean.

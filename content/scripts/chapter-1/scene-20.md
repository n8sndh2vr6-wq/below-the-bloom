The chamber opens into a vast hall. At its center sits the MAW KING upon a throne of black coral, twisted and spiked. His orange eyes glow fiercely. He watches the two Maws drag Oyster forward, pushing him to his knees.

Oyster glances around, terrified, but forces himself to meet the King’s gaze.

MAW KING (in fluent English, voice low and gravelly): You awaken in my domain, human. You have taken what is not yours.

OYSTER (voice shaking): I—my ship crashed… I’m not here to steal.

The King leans forward, studying him. Two guard-Maws stand behind, hissing softly. The purple light flickers across the King’s spines.

MAW KING: You have killed my kind. You have wounded my children. And yet… you alone stand before me.

The King’s mandibles flex thoughtfully.

MAW KING: Should I end you swiftly… or savor your terror as I have done to others?

He rises, looming over Oyster—claws clicking on the coral floor. Oyster braces himself, ready for the worst.

FADE OUT

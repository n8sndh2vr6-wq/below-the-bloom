Oyster and Chip swim slowly toward the looming gates of the castle. The massive structure rises above them in a purple hue, glowing faintly. Neither speak as the weight of their journey lingers in the silence.

OYSTER (quietly): We made it…

The castle gates creak open, responding to their presence. They exchange a look — awe, relief, disbelief.

CHIP (soft, mechanical but emotional): I believe this is what humans call… the end of the line.

OYSTER (smiling faintly): Without you, I wouldn’t have made it past the Froststalker caverns.

CHIP: And without you, I wouldn’t have existed past that shipwreck.

They walk forward through the gates.

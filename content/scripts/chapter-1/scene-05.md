The faint glow from OYSTER’s mask illuminates the drifting corpse of the berserker-class Froststalker. Blood clouds spiral upward into the dark like black ink in water. OYSTER and CHIP are swimming, slowly catching their breath, heartbeats still rattling in their chests.

OYSTER (panting): You saw that, right? I actually— we actually did that.

CHIP: That was either impressive or incredibly stupid. I haven’t decided yet.

OYSTER (still catching breath): I’ll take both. That thing nearly split me in half. I felt the heat from its breath.

CHIP: Its breath? Oyster, you don’t “feel” inanimate molecules. That was adrenaline hallucination.

OYSTER: Okay, professor. Why don’t you fight the next one?

CHIP: If I had limbs, I’d be ten times more efficient. Maybe eleven.

OYSTER (smirking): Sure, sure. You’d give it a PowerPoint and hope it dies of boredom.

CHIP: Don’t underestimate a well-timed slide transition.

They both chuckle—until they hear it.

SOUND: THUMP.

A deep, rhythmic echo in the distance. Followed by another. And another. The water trembles slightly with each one.

OYSTER (low, uneasy): What’s that?

CHIP: Could be tectonic activity. Or more company.

OYSTER: Let’s… not stick around to find out.

They swim quicker, trying not to draw attention. Then—

SOUND: WHOOSH.

A blur rushes past them. Then another. Then five more. FROSTSTALKERS—dozens of them, moving in a coordinated pack, slicing through the blackness with terrifying speed and precision. OYSTER pulls CHIP down behind a jagged, rocky outcrop. They hold still.

CHIP (whispers): They’re not even looking for us.

OYSTER: They’re hunting. But not us. Not yet.

They stay hidden, barely breathing, until the last of the pack disappears into the shadows.

CHIP: That… that was a pack. I thought they hunted solo?

OYSTER: Something’s got them scared.

CHIP: Something worse than them? Oh, great.

They cautiously push forward. As they glide through the murk, the terrain begins to shift. The dark rock gives way to jagged cliffs and crystalline frost formations. Massive, ancient bones are embedded in the walls. Blue lights shimmer faintly ahead, like scattered stars caught in glacier ice.

OYSTER (awed): The icey trenches…

CHIP: This must be it. The Froststalker kingdom.

They stop, hovering in the cold water, staring at the vast frozen canyons below. Silence. Then, a distant, resonant growl echoes up from the depths.

OYSTER (lowly): Guess we’re not done surviving yet.

Oyster and Chip are slowly swimming through a vast coral reef-like region, with bioluminescent plants pulsing gently in the current. Strange alien fish dart past. Soft beams of light shine down from above, painting the sand in patterns.

CHIP (softly, almost in awe): I have to say, for a planet that just tried to murder us… this is kind of stunning.

OYSTER (grinning): Yeah… you’d never guess what’s lurking down below. I mean—look at that plant. It’s glowing like it’s on fire.

CHIP (flashing a small hologram of its chemical makeup): Technically, it’s bioluminescent algae reacting to the salinity. But sure, “on fire” works too.

OYSTER (laughs): You ruin everything with science.

CHIP: I enhance everything with science. You’re just jealous you can’t glow.

OYSTER: Well… if I stay out here long enough I might start glowing from radiation exposure.

CHIP: That’s the spirit!

They both swim over a coral archway, and as they pass it, the terrain suddenly shifts. Ahead, the ocean floor sharply drops off into utter blackness—a yawning trench that stretches out like a mouth.

OYSTER (slowing down, voice quieting): …Oh.

CHIP: Readings just flatlined. I can’t see anything down there.

OYSTER: That’s our path, isn’t it?

CHIP: Looks like it. Welcome to the real Planet (Redacted).

The two hover at the edge, silence falling between them as the light fades away below.

FADE OUT

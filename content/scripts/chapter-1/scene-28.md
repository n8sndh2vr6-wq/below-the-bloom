CHIP (quietly, almost reverently): This is… huge. Look at all these fish. It’s like a whole other world down here.

OYSTER (eyes scanning the area, voice low): I don’t think we’re in Emergent territory anymore. This… this feels like the heart of it.

They swim out into a massive clearing. Around them, small bioluminescent fish dart through the water. Structures made from coral, ice, and salvaged metal rise like a cityscape.

CHIP (in awe): That’s… that’s a kingdom. An actual underwater kingdom.

Oyster suddenly freezes, pulling Chip behind a coral pillar. In the distance, two large figures stand surrounded by smaller creatures. One is unmistakably the Froststalker King, the other—a fish slightly larger than the others—wears a crown of coral and light.

OYSTER (whispering): That’s him. The Froststalker King. But who’s the—?

CHIP (processing rapidly): The Emergent King. Has to be. He’s wearing a crown. Also… kind of got that smug royalty look.

FROSTSTALKER KING (to the Emergent King, voice deep and resonant): They are brave. But reckless. We must warn them of what lies beyond.

EMERGENT KING (in a melodic, echoing voice): Indeed. But their survival in Maw territory is… impressive.

The kings turn as Oyster and Chip slowly approach.

OYSTER: We didn’t mean to intrude. We followed… something. We’ve been through a lot. I lost a hand.

CHIP (opening his body slightly to reveal the metal replacement): But hey, we upgraded.

FROSTSTALKER KING (nodding with surprising warmth): You fought through the dark. Faced the Maws. That alone earns respect.

EMERGENT KING (circling them gently): Many never return from Maw territory. Yet you live. You are resourceful.

OYSTER: So… this place. You’re allies?

FROSTSTALKER KING: Yes. Once enemies. Now united. We prepare each year—when the currents shift and the Maw hunger grows. It is the only way to survive.

CHIP (sarcastic): Ah, lovely. An annual war. Can’t wait for the parade.

EMERGENT KING (serious): It will come sooner than expected. The pressure stirs. The deep trembles.

FROSTSTALKER KING (steps closer, eyes grave): If you continue downward, know this—what lives beneath even us… is not like the Maws. It is older. And far more deadly.

OYSTER (quietly, but with resolve): We’re not turning back.

CHIP (sighs): Of course we’re not.

The Emergent King tilts his head, as if respecting their decision.

EMERGENT KING: Then go with caution. You carry more than just survival. The ocean watches.

The two kings step aside, revealing the path downward—dark, narrow, and pulsing faintly with the eerie purple light.

OYSTER (softly to Chip): Ready?

CHIP (flickers his lights, unfolding the blaster): Let’s go see what the deep’s really hiding.

They plunge into the descending tunnel, leaving the peaceful kingdom behind.

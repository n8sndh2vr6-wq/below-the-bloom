The Deadwake Queen floats onto a throne of chiseled obsidian coral. Her guards remain still. Oyster and Chip float before her, small in comparison.

DEADWAKE QUEEN: You will be useful. You have made it further than most prey. You have fought Maws… killed one of my kin.

OYSTER (eyes narrow): It attacked first. I didn’t sign up for any of this.

CHIP (interjects with dry sarcasm): If we could get a formal complaint form, I’m sure he’d be happy to sign it.

DEADWAKE QUEEN: Spare me. Listen. There is a war coming. One we do not intend to lose. You will lure five of my royal guards into the Emergent kingdom. You will guide them to feast. If you do not… you will not leave here alive.

OYSTER (furious): You want us to help you ambush the people who saved us? Who trusted us?

DEADWAKE QUEEN: Do not confuse sentiment with survival. Make your choice.

Oyster looks at Chip. A long silence. The pressure of the Queen’s presence is overwhelming.

CHIP: I have calculated sixty-seven escape plans in the past ten seconds. All of them end in death. Just saying.

Oyster grimaces, breath heavy.

OYSTER (quietly): We’ll do it. But when this is over… I’m coming back.

DEADWAKE QUEEN (smiles faintly, satisfied): Good. The guards will follow you. Do not fail them.

The scene ends as five massive Deadwakes rise from below, trailing behind Oyster and Chip like hungry shadows, leaving the dark kingdom behind as they begin swimming toward betrayal.

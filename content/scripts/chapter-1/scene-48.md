A sprawling room. A glowing throne carved from obsidian coral. Seated on the steps is a humanoid figure with the body of a man but the head of a squid, tendrils calmly waving in the water. He rises as they approach.

SQUID: Welcome, travelers. You’ve come far. Fought beasts. Faced fate. You may call me… Squid. Simple, yes. But powerful.

OYSTER: Are you the one who built all this?

SQUID: I am the god of this world. I made the kings. The Bloom. The tides. The food chain. You are not the first to reach me… but you are the first to survive a crash and still arrive here. That… is unique.

CHIP: Then… what happens now?

SQUID: I will revive the royals. Restore their kingdoms. Rebuild a peace that never quite existed. But… the Bloom? He locked me in this castle. Stole my power. Manipulated everything. He will not be created again.

Oyster takes a step forward, looking around the room.

OYSTER: What do you want from us?

SQUID: Nothing more. Nothing less. But…

His tone shifts.

SQUID (CONT’D): You cannot leave. Not with what you know.

He grins. His body begins to shift.

CHIP: Wait—what are you doing?

Before them, Squid transforms into Oyster. Another Chip materializes beside him. An exact copy.

SQUID: I have watched your journey. Every decision. Every victory. Every death. I know how to be you. I will go to your Earth. Be your heroes. Take your ship. Take your names. You… Will stay here. And rule this world in silence.

OYSTER: You can’t do this.

SQUID: I already am.

The doors slam shut behind them. Water churns violently as four hulking guards rise from the shadows. They grab Oyster and Chip, dragging them toward the throne.

CHIP: No! Oyster!

OYSTER: Fight—!

But the grip is too strong. They’re forced onto the throne.

SQUID: The world is changing. But the throne never does.

Everything begins to darken. The throne pulses purple. The walls ripple like liquid glass. The last thing they hear—

SQUID (V.O.): Goodbye, Oyster. Goodbye, Chip.

—is their own voices… being echoed by the copies walking away.

FADE TO BLACK

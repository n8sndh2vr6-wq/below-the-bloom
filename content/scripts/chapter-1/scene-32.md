title: Emergent Kingdom
scene: INT. EMERGENT KINGDOM – DAY (GLOWING UNDERWATER LIGHT)

summary: Oyster breaks the deal out loud. The Queen answers with war.
---

The aquatic palace glows with bioluminescent warmth. Froststalkers and Emergent creatures swim through the halls and plazas peacefully. OYSTER and CHIP swim slowly toward the gates, tension in their eyes.

CHIP (quietly): I hate this. Every second of it. We’re handing them over like… bait.

OYSTER: I know. Just… just follow my lead. We’ll fix this.

They arrive. The EMERGENT KING and FROSTSTALKER KING swim forward, their regal forms lit by the coral lights.

EMERGENT KING: Oyster! Chip! What a surprise. You return sooner than expected.

FROSTSTALKER KING: And you’ve made it out of the trenches alive. Impressive. Have you seen the Deadwakes?

Oyster’s jaw clenches. He looks over his shoulder. Behind the coral walls, barely visible, shadowy forms slither in silence—DEADWAKES.

CHIP (whispering): They’re moving…

OYSTER (loudly): Yes. We’ve seen them. (to the Kings) They’re here. Hiding behind your walls. Waiting to strike. And we were supposed to help them.

FROSTSTALKER KING (angrily): What?!

OYSTER: But we won’t. Prepare your armies. Now.

A vicious SHRIEK splits the water above them. The DEADWAKE QUEEN swoops down like a storm cloud, circling them with dark, fluid grace. Her eyes burn with betrayal.

DEADWAKE QUEEN: YOU DARE DEFY ME?! I gave you a chance. And now… you drown with them!

Before she can finish—

EMERGENT KING (roaring): Then this is WAR!

SILENCE for a beat—and then CHAOS.

DEADWAKES rain down from the dark water above, screeching and twisting, tearing through the kingdom. Froststalkers roar and clash. Emergent warriors form shields of scales. Oyster grabs his knife, and Chip shifts into battle mode.

OYSTER: Chip—go left! Protect the Emergent pods!

CHIP: Already on it!

They split. Oyster slices through the water, clashing with a Deadwake mid-spin. His oxygen blaster pulses, tearing through fins and muscle. Chip knocks back two smaller Deadwakes with an electric pulse blast.

In the background, the FROSTSTALKER KING takes down three invaders with ice-covered claws, while the EMERGENT KING summons whirlpools of pressure.

But then…

SOUND: EEEEEEYYYYYYYYYEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEEE

A horrific, echoing screech rips through the battlefield. Everyone stops. Silence falls like an anchor. Froststalkers, Deadwakes, Emergents, even the Queen—all freeze.

The water begins to glow a faint PURPLE.

Then—

CHIP (whispers): Oyster… look…

Oyster turns. In the shadows of the kingdom, ORANGE EYES blink open—dozens of them, scattered across walls, ceilings, behind coral… watching.

FROSTSTALKER KING (terrified): No…

DEADWAKE QUEEN (snapping): Who DARES interrupt this war?!

OYSTER (low, breathless): We just found the real threat.

Fade to black. The glow of orange eyes remains

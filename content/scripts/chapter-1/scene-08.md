Inside is unexpectedly spacious. Shards of the ship’s frame line the walls—fashioned into supports and platforms. Crude architecture, but clearly intentional. Glowing plants pulse with bioluminescence. The silence is thick.

OYSTER: They’re adapting. Look at this. That’s our ship’s paneling, bolted into the wall. And there—my drone arm, as a door hinge.

CHIP: This isn’t scavenging. It’s resourcefulness. Intelligence.

They move quietly through the space. At the far end, a soft light glows. A figure sits motionless on a smooth, flattened rock. It’s a Froststalker, but his frame is bulkier—his spines longer and his breathing slow. He faces a stone wall with a crude painting etched into it: a bird’s-eye view of the icy trenches.

CHIP: …Someone’s home.

OYSTER: Or a throne room.

The Froststalker slowly turns. His eyes aren’t filled with rage, but age. He speaks—not with words, but a deep, vibrating resonance that Chip’s interface automatically translates into English.

FROSTSTALKER KING: You are far from where you belong.

OYSTER (stepping forward, cautious): I’m not here to hurt anyone. We crashed. I’m just trying to survive.

FROSTSTALKER KING: Survival is not reason enough. You carry fire in your tools. Chaos in your heart. The last of your kind came here, and one by one, we buried them in the deep.

CHIP: Your people attacked us. We defended ourselves.

FROSTSTALKER KING: To you, that is truth. To us, it is history. Your ship disturbed the trench. It tore the balance. When the ice cracked, we lost many. Now we build. We defend. Even from children in suits.

OYSTER: That wasn’t our plan. I’m just trying to reach the bottom. There’s a castle—I know it sounds insane, but—

FROSTSTALKER KING (quietly): The Bloom sleeps beneath the Bloom.

A pause. The room seems to grow colder.

OYSTER: You… you know about it?

FROSTSTALKER KING: I’ve seen the glow. Felt its pulse. All kingdoms fear what stirs below. But we don’t run. We watch. We wait.

CHIP: We’re not your enemies.

FROSTSTALKER KING: Perhaps. But your presence is a flame near dry reef. I must contain it… or lose my home.

He raises his clawed hand. Two guards step out from the shadows behind them. Oyster spins, reaching for his blaster—but the King speaks again, more gently.

FROSTSTALKER KING: Not harm. Only time. You will rest, here. You will listen. And then… if your heart proves true, perhaps we can speak again.

OYSTER: So we’re prisoners?

FROSTSTALKER KING: You are guests. In chains of caution.

The guards motion them toward a smaller adjoining cave. Oyster glances at Chip, who nods reluctantly. They walk slowly into the cell, the heavy stone door closing behind them.

They fight through waves:

- FROSTSTALKERS – One joins their side after defeat, fighting beside them.
- MAWS – One nearly tears CHIP in half before being blasted away.
- DEADWAKES – Fast and brutal, the first one is slain and absorbed. The second joins them, aiding them later.

Their team grows. Names are shouted. A few die in battle, leaving heavy silence behind. But Oyster and Chip press on.

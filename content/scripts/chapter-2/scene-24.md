summary: Cornered by Acidics, Oyster gambles on the Piercer swarm beneath the sand — and Pic vouches for them.

The Acidics close in, screeching, claws glowing with burning venom. OYSTER, CHIP, and PIC stand back to back. Acid rains from the ceiling, steam hissing through the water. There's no way out…

Until Oyster spots it.

OYSTER (pointing): There! That sand — it's too smooth.

CHIP: You thinking what I'm thinking?

OYSTER: Only one way to find out.

With a defiant roar, Oyster raises his katana and slashes toward the sand. The blade slices a perfect arc. For a split second, everything is still…

Then the ground rumbles — and Piercers explode out of the sand, their screeches shaking the cavern. Acidics spin around in confusion, taken off guard.

OYSTER (shouting to Pic): Show them! Tell them you're one of them!

Pic darts forward, squealing in his sharp chirps, wings fluttering rapidly. One of the Piercers flies up and locks eyes with him… and nods.

In an instant, the Piercers go feral, diving at the Acidics. Beaks pierce, screeches disorient. The cavern becomes a warzone of shrieks and slashes.

CHIP: The exit rock!

Chip blasts it with the last charge of his oxygen gun. It weakens. A Piercer slams into it, shattering the barrier. Water rushes in.

OYSTER: Everyone out! Go!

The heroes swim through the gap as fast as they can, Piercers and Froststalkers following in chaos. Acidics try to chase, but more Piercers rise up behind them, holding the line.

Oyster grabs Pic mid-sprint and swims downward into the abyss, Chip close behind. The Froststalker King brings up the rear, his body glowing faintly as he dives deeper, protecting his people.

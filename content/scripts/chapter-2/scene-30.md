OYSTER, CHIP, PIC, the FROSTSTALKER KING, and the MAW KING catch their breath as they prepare to swim deeper. Suddenly — the ground tremors violently.

SOUND: CRACK! SPLAT!

A colossal Skywalker leg slams down, crushing Acidic corpses and even pinning a Froststalker under its chitinous foot. The heroes spin in alarm.

FROSTSTALKER KING: By the depths — look!

They crane their heads upward. Massive spider-crab legs — Skywalker legs — hammer the cavern floor in a rhythmic, earth-shaking march. Dozens of them, each as thick as a tower, blot out the soft glow above.

OYSTER: Run!

They dash amid the legs, weaving through the pillars of bone and rock. A wave of legs knocks loose boulders that tumble onto hiding Froststalkers, crushing them under glittering shards.

CHIP: We've got to move — now!

The group sprints toward the exit, but a wall of legs bars the way, forming a crushing circle around them.

MAW KING: They're corralling us!

OYSTER: Split the line!

OYSTER lunges forward and slashes one gigantic leg with his katana. The chitin cracks with a thunderous SNAP, and the circle momentarily breaks open. In an instant, the legs begin stomping and smashing everything in sight, trying to re-seal the trap.

FROSTSTALKER KING: No time — go!

The five break free, tumbling through the gap as legs pound the water around them. They swim at full speed, legs crashing and sweeping behind like wrecking balls.

SOUND: Thud… Thud… Thud…

Their path leads them into a vast chamber. In the distance, a towering stone monument glows faintly with ancient runes.

OYSTER: There—

They skid behind a coral-encrusted wall opposite the monument. The legs thunder past them, too numerous to count, and continue deeper into the cavern, their stomps fading.

Silence returns, broken only by the heroes' ragged breathing. OYSTER, CHIP, and PIC exchange determined looks. Pic squeaks.

OYSTER: We made it. Now… the monument awaits.

CHIP: Together.

They share a firm nod — united, battered, but unbroken — before swimming out from cover toward their destiny.

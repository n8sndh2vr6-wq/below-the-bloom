Black water churns. Acid burns through coral and ruins alike. The ACIDICS descend, tall and grotesque, flexing their scorched hands, hissing through teeth hidden in petal-like mouths.

OYSTER grips his katana tightly. CHIP checks the charge on his mini blaster. PIC hovers beside them, wings buzzing faintly, eyes locked on the monsters.

OYSTER: We don't kill. We stall. Hold 'em.

The Acidics charge. One leaps forward — Oyster parries with his blade, narrowly dodging a swipe of sizzling fingers. The acid singes his shoulder armor.

CHIP: They're faster than before!

Chip blasts at the ground, creating bubbles and debris to obscure the enemy's vision. Another Acidic lunges, and Pic — tiny but fearless — dives under it, latching onto one of its legs and spinning it into another. They crash into a pillar with a deafening THUD.

OYSTER (gasping): Pic?!

But Pic is already back in formation, wings buzzing, acid dripping harmlessly from his fur thanks to his slick hide. He lets out a sharp squeak, doing a fast barrel roll through two Acidic legs, disorienting them.

Oyster stabs his katana into a crumbling column to knock it down in front of a charging Acidic, and Chip zips around another, drawing fire. The Acidics hiss and flail, confused by the trio's relentless movement and coordination.

CHIP: Since when can Pic fight like that?!

OYSTER (grinning): He learns fast.

A moment later, a massive sound echoes through the water — a deep, vibrating screech. The Piercers are gone.

The Acidics freeze, sensing the shift in balance. Oyster, Chip, and Pic take their chance.

OYSTER: GO!

They all dart backward into the deep. The Froststalkers are already in motion, sweeping past like shadows, leading the charge into the black depths.

OYSTER, CHIP, and PIC dive after them, breathing hard, the glow of battle fading behind them as the wasteland vanishes into silence.

title: Whispers of Power
scene: EXT. FLOATING AT THE SHIPWRECK – NIGHT
act: power
number: 4
summary: Oyster convinces Chip to go looking for Squid's "ancient powers" before Squid can use them first.
---

OYSTER and CHIP rest on the shattered remains of their ship. The moon glistens on the water as waves roll calmly.

CHIP: You think we're really done?

OYSTER: Not even close.

CHIP: What do you mean?

OYSTER: Remember what Squid said, before he vanished — "the ancient powers." He said they were real. Buried. Hidden. Probably waiting for someone dumb enough to go looking.

CHIP: You think that's us?

OYSTER: I think that has to be us. You saw what he became with them… We can't let that happen again.

CHIP: We just got back, Oyster. Air's fresh. Water's calm.

OYSTER: Exactly. That peace? It won't last if Squid ever comes back. We need to be ready.

CHIP (sighs, nodding): Alright. Let's finish what we started.

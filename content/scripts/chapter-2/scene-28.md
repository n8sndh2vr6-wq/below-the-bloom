The dust from the battle still hangs in the water. OYSTER and CHIP rush over to the crumpled figure of the MAW KING, his chest leaking dark blue ichor.

OYSTER: Hold on, we've got you.

CHIP morphs back into his toolbox form, clanking open. Oyster grabs a thick tube of filler paste from within and applies it into the gaping wound. The paste sizzles as it knits the flesh together.

MAW KING (wheezing): I… made a promise, didn't I?

OYSTER (nodding): And you kept it. Just hang in there.

Meanwhile, PIC hurries over to the FROSTSTALKER KING, who is slowly trying to rise. PIC grabs a chunk of the Piercer Queen's blood-soaked wing, dips it like a brush, and carefully glues the shattered crown back together. He nudges it into the King's hand and gives a soft, proud squeak.

The Froststalker King looks down, touched, and places the broken crown back on his head — as crooked and scarred as he is.

Suddenly—

SOUND: SHING!

A gleam flashes across the Queen's corpse. Standing atop her body is a SILHOUETTED FIGURE, katana as long as a spear in his hands. In one clean motion, he slices the Piercer Queen's head off, the body giving one last twitch.

OYSTER (stunned): Who… who are you?

The figure doesn't move. His blade lowers slowly, and his deep, inhumanly low voice fills the chamber:

SILHOUETTED FIGURE: I'm here for reasons… very confidential.

OYSTER (stepping forward): That's not an answer. You've been following us. What do you want?

A pause. The figure doesn't respond.

CHIP's lights blaze suddenly, illuminating the figure — revealing all-black armor, soaking wet, a jagged hood, and a face hidden behind a dark breathing mask. Strange symbols pulse faintly across his chest and sleeves.

He looks… human.

OYSTER (quietly): You're… one of us?

The figure doesn't speak. Instead—

SOUND: SLASH!

In an instant, he cuts CHIP's screen, shattering it and sending sparks flying. CHIP collapses, lights out, motionless.

OYSTER: CHIP!

He lunges forward but the figure is already backing away, blade pointed downward.

SILHOUETTED FIGURE: Stay out of my way.

And with that, the figure disappears into the dark — his silhouette vanishing behind a rocky outcropping, leaving silence and confusion in his wake.

OYSTER kneels by CHIP, gripping his shattered form. PIC stares, eyes wide with horror. The FROSTSTALKER KING and MAW KING both watch in grim silence.

OYSTER (softly): What is this world becoming?

The water grows darker as OYSTER and CHIP swim downward, entering the familiar crevice where light once guided them years ago. The bioluminescent glow from deep-sea flora flickers faintly around them.

CHIP (quietly): This is it… looks the same.

OYSTER: Yeah. Just colder somehow.

As they swim into the Maw Kingdom, they're greeted by silence — until distant figures begin to appear. MAWS slowly emerge from the dark, their glowing eyes wide as they spot the two familiar figures swimming through.

No one speaks. The tension is thick. Maws freeze in place, unsure if what they're seeing is real.

CHIP (whispers): They remember.

OYSTER: Of course they do.

They make their way through the city, passing motionless Maws, and arrive at the Maw King's cave. The cave is lit dimly by glowing roots. On the floor, in the corner, OYSTER's old severed hand bones still rest.

The MAW KING has his back to them, hunched over. He speaks without turning around.

MAW KING (softly): You're late.

He turns. His eyes widen as he sees OYSTER and CHIP standing there. A slow grin forms across his jagged face.

MAW KING (stepping forward): By the oceans… You're alive.

OYSTER: We never died. We were trapped. Squid locked us in the castle for two years.

CHIP: We only just got out. Everything's different.

The MAW KING looks at them with a mix of disbelief and admiration. He glances over at the bones, then back at OYSTER.

MAW KING: We thought you were gone for good. After everything we fought through… we buried you with stories.

OYSTER: What happened here?

MAW KING (serious now): The Emergents… gone. Died out a year after the Bloom. The Deadwakes… imploded from the inside. Their own rage ate them alive. The Froststalkers? Vanished. Kingdom's empty. No one knows why.

CHIP: We were just there. Only scraps and ghosts.

MAW KING: Only us now. Maws stayed. Watched the world rot.

He steps closer.

MAW KING: Where are you headed?

OYSTER: We're going after the powers Squid talked about. If they're real… we're gonna find them.

MAW KING (nods, quietly): Then I'll follow. Stay behind. Silent. If you need me, I'll be there.

CHIP: You sure?

MAW KING: Two days. That's all I need. I'm part of a search team — looking for the Froststalker remnants. Once I report back, I'll vanish with the tides.

OYSTER: Deal.

They clasp forearms — the first time in years. A silent bond reforged.

The scene fades as OYSTER and CHIP leave the cave, the MAW KING watching them go, a strange hope glinting in his eye.

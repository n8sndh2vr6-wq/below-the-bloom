But the kingdom is gone. In its place — a wasteland. Broken spires. Craters. Hundreds of creatures — Piercers, rogue species, corrupted mutations — descend from every direction.

The FROSTSTALKER KING stands tall atop a broken tower, his massive claws glowing with ancient light. A full Froststalker army assembles at his sides.

OYSTER, CHIP, and PIC join him at the front lines. The Acidic walks out of the cave behind them — joined now by more. Dozens. Hundreds.

CHIP: It's happening.

OYSTER: It always was.

PIC squeaks, puffing up beside them, wings ready.

FROSTSTALKER KING: We hold the line.

OYSTER draws his katana.

OYSTER: Then let's begin.

The horizon darkens as the hoard charges—

FADE TO BLACK.

---
title: A Story Interrupted
scene: INT. STONE CAVES – NIGHT
act: reunion
place: icy-trenches
number: 15
summary: Telling Pic how they got here is cut short when an Acidic finds the cave.
---

The quiet hum of water currents lulls the cave. Inside, OYSTER, CHIP, and PIC rest in a circle. The glow from bioluminescent stones casts soft purples and greens on their faces.

CHIP: You know… it's strange. After all we've seen… this cave feels like the first moment of peace in years.

OYSTER: Yeah. But I can't stop thinking about how we got here.

Pic squeaks quietly, listening intently.

CHIP (smiling at Pic): You want to know, little guy?

Pic squeaks again, slightly louder this time. He curls his wings around himself like a blanket, blinking slowly.

OYSTER: Alright then. I guess we'll tell you. From the beginning.

He exhales and begins—

OYSTER (CONT'D): We crashed. The ship went down in the wrong part of the ocean. Real wrong. We were attacked. Hunted.

CHIP: We lost people. We found kingdoms. The Froststalkers helped us. The Maws… they took my hand.

OYSTER: And Squid… he tricked us. Lied about everything. We got trapped in a castle — trapped for years. Alone.

Pic lets out a slow, sad squeak. He nestles closer to OYSTER, pressing his forehead against his arm.

CHIP: But we're here now. Still moving forward.

Suddenly — a cold click echoes from behind them. A slow wet squish — and something steps into the cave entrance.

OYSTER (whispering): Someone's here.

All three freeze. From the shadows, a tall figure unfurls — an ACIDIC. Its black, bony skin shines with dark mucus. No eyes. No mouth — until it peels open like a demonic flower. Its hands slowly unroll into grotesque burning tendrils, oozing and hissing as they meet the cave walls. The rock begins to melt and steam.

CHIP: Move!

Pic lets out a terrified screech as the trio jumps back. The cave shakes — then a low roar booms outside. Another. Then another.

OYSTER: The Froststalkers.

They rush out of the collapsing cave into the open.

The group slows, the chaos above muffled. Only their breathing, and the slow drift of the water remains. It's calm. For now.

OYSTER (to Pic): You did good, little guy.

Pic squeaks proudly, nestling against his shoulder. Chip wipes acid from his arm armor, wincing.

CHIP: That was too close.

They look ahead — into the unknown black below them.

OYSTER: Then let's find what we came here for.

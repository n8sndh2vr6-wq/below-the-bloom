OYSTER, CHIP, and PIC swim excitedly down the ancient corridor. The glowing walls pulse with the same violet hue as the orb. PIC lets out a joyous squeak.

OYSTER: Can you believe it, Chip? From crashing in that black ocean to this moment…

CHIP: I'd say the crash was the low point, but everything after that's been one wild ride.

OYSTER: I loved the Froststalkers — first taste of kindness in a world of monsters.

CHIP: Maws were brutal but honorable. Then Squid locked us away…

OYSTER: Emergents… Deadwakes… Piercers… Acidics… we fought them all.

CHIP: And lost more than we gained. But we survived.

They reach the end of the hallway. The massive stone doors groan open into a vast grand chamber. At its center, a pedestal holds a glowing purple orb.

OYSTER: This is it!

They dart forward—

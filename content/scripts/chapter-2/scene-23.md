More Acidics pour in. Dozens. Furious. The room seals. The exit collapses behind them.

OYSTER: We're trapped!

The acid drips from the walls. Pic is squealing, clinging to Oyster's back. Chip fires at the ceiling but it just burns more acid down. Oyster's armor hisses as acid eats away at it.

CHIP: We've got nowhere to go!

The trio braces themselves as Acidics close in from all sides, their hands unfurling, the hiss of death all around.

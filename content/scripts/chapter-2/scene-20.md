Darkness rushes past. OYSTER, CHIP, PIC, and the FROSTSTALKER ARMY swim at full speed, shadows weaving between jagged rock walls. Acid streams sizzle through the water like toxic meteors.

Behind them, the Acidics pursue — relentless and enraged. Every shriek echoes like underwater thunder. One drops from above, melting a Froststalker mid-dive. Another falls beside Pic, barely missing. The team swerves.

OYSTER: They're closing in!

CHIP: Dead end ahead!

Chip pulls out his sonar scanner — a solid wall of jagged rock looms in their path.

CHIP (CONT'D): I've got a plan. Hide behind that outcrop — let 'em pass us.

OYSTER: What about the Froststalkers?

CHIP: They'll get cornered… that's when we come back in. One big shot — buy them time.

The Froststalker King hears this, slowing ever so slightly, giving a subtle nod. Trust.

The crowd of Froststalkers stirs and parts as OYSTER, CHIP, and PIC push through, chasing a familiar silhouette. But when they reach the center—

It's gone.

Then — a booming female voice echoes through the cave, ancient and resonant.

PIERCER QUEEN (O.S.): My child is amongst you. Return him… or suffer the consequences.

The cavern falls dead silent. Even the water seems to freeze. Pic lets out a sharp, quiet chirp. Recognition. Fear.

A massive shadow descends from the ceiling — wings outstretched, beak like a spear, white fur glowing faintly against the darkness. The Piercer Queen lands, her presence dominating the cavern.

She locks eyes with Pic. He trembles, stumbling back.

PIERCER QUEEN: You belong to me, child. Come home.

Pic squeals and, in desperation, opens his ribcage in a mimicry of intimidation. The Queen only laughs, the sound slicing through the cave.

PIERCER QUEEN: I made you. You're not one of them.

Before anyone can react, the Froststalker King steps forward, holding his shattered crown with dignity.

FROSTSTALKER KING: He stays with us.

SOUND: CRACK!

The Queen slaps him aside with one thunderous wing, sending him flying into a jagged rock wall. His crown splits in two. Blood seeps into the water.

PIERCER QUEEN (sneering): Royalty? Not in my eyes.

She turns back to Pic, advancing. But — OYSTER steps in front of him, katana drawn. CHIP hovers at his side, his body shifting into oxygen pressure blaster form, humming with energy.

OYSTER: If you want him… you'll have to go through us.

The Queen pauses. A cold smile crawls across her beak.

PIERCER QUEEN (sneering): How sweet. You named him. You think that makes him yours?

She flares her wings, dozens of Piercers emerging behind her from the walls and shadows, eyes glowing.

Pic backs into Oyster's leg, squeaking quietly — but determined.

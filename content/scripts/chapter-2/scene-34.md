---
title: The Duel for the Orb
scene: INT. MONUMENT CHAMBER – CONTINUOUS
act: monument
number: 34
summary: Pic, Chip, and Oyster fight Mach to a standstill at the foot of the pedestal.
---

Mach's long katana glints in the purple light as he lunges at Pic, aiming for the exposed, fiery ribcage. Pic reacts instantly — his own flame erupts, engulfing Mach's blade. The metal hisses and warps into molten sludge on the floor.

MACH: You'll burn with it!

Pic squeaks defiantly. CHIP morphs into his force-field form, projecting a glowing bubble around Pic. Mach hurls a flurry of throwing knives toward Pic's flaming chest — they clatter harmlessly off the field. One knife spins back and nearly lodges in Mach's knee.

CHIP: You picked the wrong target!

Oyster pushes himself up, dizzy but determined, clutching his acid pressure blaster. He fires rapid shots at Mach, acid streams sizzling across the tiled floor.

OYSTER: End this!

Mach charges Pic again. Pic leaps aside, sweeping a wall of fire behind him that trips Mach, sending him sprawling. He scrambles upright, yanks a single throwing knife — and hurls it at CHIP's exposed control screen. It shatters. CHIP's lights flicker off; he powers down.

OYSTER: Chip!

Mach snarls and manipulates CHIP's remaining current, creating a magnetic pull that drags him toward Pic's blazing heart. Encouraged, Mach delivers a brutal punch to Pic's face. Pic yelps.

Oyster lunges, blade raised — but Mach deflects the strike, strokes the katana across Oyster's hand, carving a deep wound.

MACH: Pathetic!

Seizing the moment, Pic darts forward and ignites Mach's leg with his inner flame. Mach howls as fire climbs his chitin. Oyster turns and sprints for the glowing orb on its pedestal — but Mach fires a hidden crossbow, mortaring the pedestal's base. The blast sends Mach sliding across the chamber to a corner.

Oyster spins and fires his acid blaster through the flame — acid droplets arc toward Mach but he deftly fires the crossbow again, this time striking Oyster's shoulder. Acid and fire cascade over Oyster's suit; he grits his teeth as the corrosion eats at metal.

Mach collapses, fire sizzling down his limbs, screaming in agony. Oyster, choking on acid fumes, crawls forward toward the orb.

From Mach's belt, he whips out a small vial of pills. With shaking fingers, he pops one. Instantly, a wave of freezing ice radiates out, coating the chamber and extinguishing flames and acid. The ice cracks and shatters, leaving Mach and Oyster both freed from burning and choking.

OYSTER (grimly): Your moves…

Both sprint the final yards. Mach lunges with his katana—

OYSTER: Not today!

Oyster pivots and punches Mach low — right between the legs. Mach doubles over in shock. Oyster reaches the pedestal, places a hand on the glowing purple orb—

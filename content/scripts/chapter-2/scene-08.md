---
title: Naming Pic
scene: INT. DEEPER OCEAN – DESCENDING TOWARD THE UNKNOWN – CONTINUOUS
act: piercers
number: 8
summary: They name their new companion mid-swim — and a swarm tries to take him back.
---

OYSTER, CHIP, and the baby Piercer swim downward, the water getting darker and the pressure intensifying.

CHIP: They came out of nowhere. If we were a second slower…

OYSTER: It was an ambush. Textbook. The kind you don't survive twice.

The baby Piercer lets out a soft squeak, floating just between them, its wings twitching shyly. It seems to follow the conversation, turning toward each speaker.

OYSTER (staring at it curiously): You really listening to us?

The Piercer squeaks in reply.

CHIP: Creepy smart… like it understands everything.

OYSTER: Alright, little one… you need a name. Can't keep calling you "it."

The Piercer squeaks again, short and bright.

OYSTER: Okay, okay… let's see. Spike?

Silence.

OYSTER: Winger?

A small buzz from the Piercer's wings.

OYSTER: Pic?

The Piercer lets out an explosive string of excited squeaks, wings flapping, swimming in small circles.

CHIP (eyes wide): Okay, okay! Shhh! You're gonna—

A distant, rushing screech cuts through the deep. The currents stir. A massive swarm of Piercers flies past them like a storm cloud.

CHIP: Too late.

The swarm splits, half surrounding Oyster and Chip, cutting them off from Pic. The other half grabs Pic gently, protectively — but it squawks and starts biting their toes violently. One yelps and drops it.

OYSTER: Pic!

OYSTER spins and fires his oxygen pressure blaster — a huge force of compressed air blasts open a tunnel through the swarm. One of the Piercers darts through the blast just before it closes, slashing its beak through the side of the blaster — cracking and breaking it for good.

CHIP: The blaster!

OYSTER turns with rage burning in his eyes, his ear still damaged, the world spinning slightly.

OYSTER (snarling): You've done enough.

He grabs the attacking Piercer by the neck, pulling it close, their eyes locked.

OYSTER: You tried to take him.

He screams into its face, shaking with fury. Then, in one clean motion, unsheathes his katana and slices its head clean off.

The body sinks slowly into the depths. For a moment — silence.

Then, Pic floats up next to them, wings low, eyes wide but… unaware. It snuggles up against OYSTER's shoulder, chirping gently.

CHIP: He didn't see…

OYSTER nods slowly, resheathing the katana.

OYSTER: Let's move.

They turn, the three of them — OYSTER, CHIP, and Pic — descending deeper into the abyss.

KILL: Oyster > Piercer raider | katana beheading, defending Pic

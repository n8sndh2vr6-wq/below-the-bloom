Everything is dark. No light, no sound. Just an overwhelming feeling of descent. Suddenly — a beam of light cuts through the black.

SOUND: CLICK.

OYSTER (into the void): Chip?

CHIP (O.S.): I'm here!

OYSTER turns to see CHIP ignite his own light. As both beams sweep through the space, they see — a massive pit of bubbling acid beneath them. They are falling — fast.

CHIP: No no no — he has acid in his body?!

OYSTER: Guess that's why no one made it out alive…

CHIP (shouting): Grab Pic! NOW! Use your katana — jam it into the wall! HOLD ON!

OYSTER reaches out and snatches PIC, who is flailing helplessly. With his other hand, he pulls out his katana and drives it with all his strength into the fleshy wall of the King's throat.

OYSTER: Got it!

The katana sinks deep into the pulsing wall. OYSTER catches CHIP with his foot, pinning him to the blade's hilt as all three dangle, suspended above the boiling acid.

Steam rises from below, burning their faces. PIC clutches OYSTER, shaking.

CHIP (looking down): This is not how I pictured going out.

OYSTER: We're not dying in a digestive system.

He pulls his oxygen pressure blaster from his side — its casing cracked from before.

OYSTER: Blaster's shot. But maybe… if we hit the muscle just right—

CHIP: You thinking what I'm thinking?

OYSTER: Always.

CHIP: Let's make this giant regret his appetite.

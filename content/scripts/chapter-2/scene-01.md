Dim purple light filters through cracks in the towering castle walls. OYSTER and CHIP lie asleep in the throne room, slouched against the steps of the throne where they've spent countless nights. A low creak echoes.

SOUND: Stone grinding, slow and ancient — the castle doors opening by themselves.

CHIP stirs first, eyes blinking open. OYSTER jolts awake soon after. They lock eyes, then slowly stand, glancing toward the hallway.

CHIP (whispering): Did you hear that?

OYSTER: The doors…

They carefully approach the threshold of the throne room. As they peek through, they catch a glimpse of a shadow — a slick, long form dashing silently past the corridor.

CHIP: Was that… someone?

OYSTER: Or something.

The Piercer Queen perches back on a jagged rock, wings spread lazily — as if it were a throne. Her voice is silk and venom.

PIERCER QUEEN: Kill them.

With a shrill screech, dozens of Piercers erupt forward, bones exposed, screeching, claws extended. The cavern explodes into chaos.

OYSTER swings his katana, slicing through two Piercers as CHIP, fully transformed into his blaster form, fires rapid oxygen bursts, knocking enemies back. PIC, small but ferocious, flips and dodges, slicing at Piercers with his jagged baby ribs. He's not just surviving — he's fighting.

All around them, Froststalkers try to hold their own — but it's clear: this is a losing battle. One by one, the Queen's Piercers either fall or retreat, until only silence remains. Then—

SOUND: WHOOSH!

The Queen lifts herself from her throne, her wings flaring wide — and slams them down in one motion. A massive shockwave blasts through the battlefield, hurling Froststalkers, bodies, debris — all gone in an instant.

Only Oyster, Chip, and Pic remain, struggling to stay upright as the Queen looms over them. She lifts one massive wing — about to crush them all—

SOUND: THWACK!

Something dashes past them in a blur and slams a foot right into the Queen's face, sending her skidding back. Glowing orange eyes gleam in the dust. The Maw King.

MAW KING: Couldn't find the Froststalkers… Turns out they were here all along.

Before she recovers, the Maw King launches forward, piercing his claw straight through her eye socket. She lets out a roar of pure rage.

OYSTER's eyes widen — just like the Deadwake Queen. Same move. Same king.

The Piercer Queen rears back and opens her ribcage, massive and spiraling like a skeletal flower. The Maw King slips, nearly falling in—!

OYSTER reacts fast, loading acid into his broken blaster. He fires at her feet, melting the rock and throwing her off balance. She snarls and flaps her wings, lifting into the air, finally managing to shake off the Maw King. With a vicious swoop, she plunges her claw into his chest — a clean puncture.

The Maw King crashes down, wheezing — barely alive.

The Queen turns her attention back to Oyster, grinning — until he suddenly leaps, climbing inside her still-open chest cavity — and fires his acid pressure blaster.

SOUND: BOOM!

Her ribcage jerks open wider, smoke and green liquid pouring out. Her scream is deafening, animalistic, primal — then— she falls. Still. Dead.

The Queen of the Piercers is no more.

Oyster crawls out of her chest, splattered and breathing heavy, just as Chip and Pic help him up. The battlefield is silent but for the dying groans of the wounded.

OYSTER (quietly): It's over.

summary: Chip comes back online just in time for another Acidic wave, and Oyster's fury finally shows its teeth.
---

OYSTER kneels beside the fallen CHIP, sparks still flickering from his shattered casing. PIC hovers anxiously overhead.

OYSTER: You hang on, buddy. I'm an engineer — I can fix you.

He opens CHIP's casing, exposing a tangle of wires and circuits. Carefully, he realigns connectors and seals panels. With a final spark—

CHIP (voice crackling back to life): Systems nominal. Power at thirty-two percent. Thanks, pal.

OYSTER wipes sweat from his brow. CHIP's eyepiece flickers on, bathing the cave in soft light.

CHIP (CONT'D): My light has an X-ray mode. I scanned that figure. Its bone structure… identical to a human's, but with a squid-like head. Yet — its build is slimmer and shorter than mine.

OYSTER: So… not Squid. Not you. Someone new.

CHIP: Affirmative. But it's about your size. And it moved like you… almost.

Before they can speculate further, a roar echoes from the cavern's entrance.

FROSTSTALKER KING (O.S.): Acidics! They're back!

The MAW KING, now patched and grimacing, rises alongside the FROSTSTALKER KING. The four heroes — OYSTER, CHIP now in blaster mode, PIC, and the two kings — form a battered line as Acidics pour into the chamber.

One lunges; Oyster parries with his katana, sparks flying as blade meets corrosive tendrils. Another strikes Chip; Chip fires a pulse that knocks it off balance. Pic darts between legs, scratching with razor-sharp baby ribs. The Froststalker King rips one Acidic in two with an ice-covered claw. The Maw King charges another, battering it with seismic blows. Acid drips, bubbles hiss, and the chamber shakes under the onslaught.

At first, the Acidics overwhelm them — numbers swarming, corrosive hands everywhere. The heroes are forced back toward the cavern's center, inch by inch.

OYSTER: Fall back — cover me!

Amidst the chaos, OYSTER spots the silhouetted figure standing on a ledge above, watching impassively. He remembers the figure's attack on CHIP — the shattered screen, the blade at his throat. The moment snaps something fierce in him. He drops his guard, fury overtaking caution.

OYSTER: Pic — get down!

He surges forward alone, dispatching Acidics in rapid succession — one stabbed through the ribs with his katana, another swung by the arm into a rock pillar, a third blasted point-blank with acid. His movements are savage, precise — each strike fueled by rage and fear for Chip.

CHIP: Oyster! You can't—

But Oyster doesn't stop. He cleaves a path straight toward the silhouette's shadow at the back of the cavern. Acidics topple like statues.

The heroes regroup. The Acidics lie broken, melting back into the shadows. PIC lands on Oyster's shoulder, chirping in awe. CHIP powers down his blaster, drained but operational.

OYSTER (grim, breathing heavy): We can't wait for help. We make our own path.

He turns to the kings, who watch him with respect and slight fear.

FROSTSTALKER KING: Your courage… is greater than any of ours.

MAW KING: You fight like the ocean itself.

OYSTER: Then let's find that monument.

They swim deeper into the darkness — united, battle-scarred, and unyielding.

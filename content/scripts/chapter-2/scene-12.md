---
title: A Rocket Made of Acid
scene: INT. INSIDE THE EMERGENT KING – ABOVE THE ACID PIT – CONTINUOUS
act: emergent
number: 12
summary: Oyster free-falls, then fires himself back up through the King's own throat to blast them free.
---

OYSTER clings to the katana embedded in the fleshy wall. CHIP dangles by one hand, PIC squeaking in fear and curling close to OYSTER's chest.

OYSTER: Chip — grab Pic and get him onto the hilt. Keep him safe.

CHIP: What are you doing?

OYSTER: Something stupid. Now go!

CHIP climbs up quickly, taking PIC and carefully perching him on the flat handle of the katana. PIC squeaks nervously as CHIP steadies him.

OYSTER looks down — acid boils beneath him. He closes his eyes, breathes deep — then lets go.

CHIP: OYSTER — NO!

OYSTER free-falls down. At the last second, he flips, pulls his damaged oxygen pressure blaster, scoops up a swirling wave of acid in the chamber, and fires straight upward. The blast launches him up like a rocket. The force tears through the inside of the King's throat. Acid sprays behind him like a comet trail.

OYSTER rockets past CHIP and PIC, grabs the katana, then flips and lands back beside them, coughing.

OYSTER (grinning through pain): Told you. Not dying in a digestive system.

CHIP: You're insane!

OYSTER: Grab the katana and Pic. Follow my lead.

OYSTER fires another blast — acid-loaded — at the King's massive teeth near the throat's exit. The acid sizzles and melts into the bone-like structures. A tremendous roar echoes from deep within. The King's mouth flings open in pain — water and acid gush outward in a violent current.

SOUND: WHOOSH!

OYSTER, CHIP, and PIC are blasted through the tunnel, shooting back through the hole they entered from, water screaming around them as they tumble end over end into the open ocean again.

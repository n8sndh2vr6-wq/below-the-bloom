They dive deep again, pushing through dark waters. The once-mighty Froststalker Kingdom is now in ruins — quiet, lifeless. Towers collapsed. Structures cracked.

A few FROSTSTALKERS skitter by, no longer in patrol formations. They silently gather scrap metal, relics, and fragments, then vanish back into the darkness one by one.

CHIP: Where is everyone?

OYSTER: Something's wrong… they're hiding. Or running.

Suddenly, a shadowy figure darts past just beyond a crumbled tower. The two freeze, staring at where it vanished behind a jagged rock.

CHIP: There it is again!

OYSTER: We follow?

CHIP: We always do.

They swim slowly toward the rock — tense, cautious. OYSTER peeks around the edge. Nothing. No figure. Just open, empty blackness.

CHIP: Gone…

OYSTER: Not gone. Waiting.

They turn back toward where the Froststalkers had been — but they're all gone too. The ocean feels heavier now. Quiet.

CHIP: They left us.

OYSTER: Then we go deeper.

SCENE: EXT. DEEPER TRENCH – MOMENTS LATER

They descend together, side by side. No turning back. The last flickers of the Froststalker Kingdom vanish behind them, swallowed by the depths.

FADE OUT.

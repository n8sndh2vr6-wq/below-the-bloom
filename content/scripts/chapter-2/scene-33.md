title: An Old Enemy's Blade
scene: INT. GRAND CHAMBER – CONTINUOUS
act: monument
number: 33
summary: Oyster is stabbed from behind, and the silhouette finally introduces himself — Mach.
---

As OYSTER lunges for the orb, a katana hilt suddenly protrudes from his chest.

SOUND: SLASH!

OYSTER's eyes widen. He stares at the blade, then collapses forward into CHIP's arms.

CHIP transforms instantly into toolbox form, flipping open panels and revealing medic tools. He frantically applies patches to OYSTER's wound.

CHIP: Hang on! I — I didn't see who did this!

Meanwhile, PIC swims toward the pedestal—

SOUND: CLINK CLINK CLINK!

A rain of throwing knives descends from the ceiling, slicing through columns and embedding in the walls around PIC. He leaps back with a squeal of alarm.

The silhouetted figure steps into the orb's light. He removes his hood, revealing a tall, slim figure with a human body and a squid head, eyes dark and unreadable. Pic squeaks, terrified.

OYSTER stirs, wounded but conscious.

OYSTER: Who…

MACH: Call me Mach — short for "Machete."

He steps forward, katana in hand.

MACH (CONT'D): I'm a bounty hunter, part of Squid's secret assassination order. Squid sent me… to kill you two. And I'm quite eager to finish the job… especially the little one.

He eyes PIC with cruel amusement. PIC, trembling, opens his chest; a burning ember glows within his ribcage. Mach's slit-like eyes narrow in recognition.

MACH: Of course… an Apex-Berserker Piercer.

He raises his katana, ready to strike. The chamber falls into tense silence as Mach advances.

---
title: The King's Last Stand
scene: INT. DEEP TRENCH – APPROACHING THE MONUMENT – NIGHT
act: monument
number: 31
summary: The Acidic King corners them at the monument's foot, and the Froststalker King pays the final price.
---

OYSTER, CHIP (in blaster form), PIC, the FROSTSTALKER KING, and the MAW KING swim in tight formation toward the glowing stone monument ahead. The water is thick with tension.

CHIP: We're almost there — just past this rock ridge.

Suddenly — the ACIDIC KING plummets from the darkness above, landing with enough force to crack the cavern floor.

SOUND: CRASH!

ACIDIC KING (voice booming): All my troops… dead. Skywalkers crushed them. And you — threaten my species. I must kill you.

He lunges. OYSTER parries the massive maw-slit mouth with the flat of his katana, pushing the Acidic King backward.

ACIDIC KING (folding his scarred arms): No one stands in my way!

He unfurls his acidic tendrils, hissing as they reach for PIC. OYSTER shouts—

OYSTER: Pic — get down!

But PIC boldly opens his rib cage, then darts forward and stabs the Acidic King's arm, claws biting deep into burning flesh.

MAW KING: I have to do this!

The MAW KING leaps up, plunging his claw through where an eye would be if the Acidic King had one. The Acidic King roars. He whips around on his six legs and kicks OYSTER, CHIP, and PIC aside with his back pair of legs, sending them crashing into stalagmites.

The FROSTSTALKER KING surges back into the fray, slashing the Acidic King's underbelly with his fin, blood clouding the water.

ACIDIC KING (through gritted teeth): Pathetic…

He sweeps a hand and coats OYSTER's pressure suit in corrosive poison. OYSTER's suit hisses and seals break — he begins to suffocate.

OYSTER (voice fading): I… can't… breathe…

CHIP: Hold on, buddy!

CHIP fires his oxygen blaster into a massive bubble around OYSTER, stabilizing his air supply.

ACIDIC KING: You think you win?

He grabs OYSTER by the shoulder and drags him away from the bubble. OYSTER flails as the Acidic King bares his jagged maw. Just then — PIC lets out a piercing screech. The Acidic King's echolocation trembles, sending him into a blind spasm of agony.

ACIDIC KING (voice cracking): No… not…

He squeezes his eyes shut — then, in a last desperate act, lunges at the MAW KING, knocking him off a nearby ledge. The MAW KING plunges out of sight, unconscious.

The Acidic King spins toward CHIP, unleashing a spray of acid that shorts out CHIP's systems. CHIP collapses, dark.

Pic hisses and darts forward, piercing one of the Acidic King's legs with his beak. The Acidic King howls — and dives into the sand to recover.

Now only OYSTER and the FROSTSTALKER KING remain standing, both battered. The Acidic King emerges, arms folded back into lethal tendrils, and pummels toward OYSTER.

At the last moment, the FROSTSTALKER KING launches himself and the Acidic King upward, impaling both on the cavern's central spire at the top of the monument. The King's sacrifice is instant and fatal — the Acidic King's body falls limp.

OYSTER (staring in shock): NO — Your Majesty!

He scrambles to the spire, but it's too late. The FROSTSTALKER KING's final breath ripples through the water.

Slowly, CHIP, PIC, and the MAW KING (bleeding but alive) return, eyes wide with horror.

OYSTER (voice breaking): He… he saved us.

MAW KING (firmly): I must tell my kingdom…

Before OYSTER can respond, the MAW KING swims away without warning, leaving OYSTER, CHIP, and PIC alone.

They hover beside the broken spire, the monument glowing above, the weight of sacrifice heavy around them.

OYSTER (sotto): For him… we finish this.

PIC chirps softly, and CHIP's lights reawaken faintly. The three of them turn toward the monument — determined, grief-stricken, and ready.

KILL: The Froststalker King > The Acidic King | impaled both of them on the monument's central spire, a fatal sacrifice

FADE OUT.

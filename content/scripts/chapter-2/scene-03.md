title: Breaking the Surface
scene: EXT. SURFACE – NIGHT

summary: Two years under, and they finally breathe open air beside the wreck of the ship that brought them here.
---

The surface breaks with a splash as the two rise from the deep. They breach, gasping in unison.

SOUND: A long inhale, the rush of air.

Floating nearby: the twisted wreckage of their old ship, its frame bent and half-sunken. Moonlight casts a silvery glow over the water.

CHIP: It's still here…

OYSTER: Just like us.

They swim to the wreckage and rest their arms on it, breathing heavily but smiling faintly.

CHIP: You think Earth even remembers we were gone?

OYSTER: I don't know. But we remember it. And now… we've got work to do.

They float in silence, staring out at the open horizon, waves gently lapping against them.

FADE OUT.

The armies gather under the broken spires. The hoard of Piercers and Acidics creeps forward like a living tide. The atmosphere crackles with tension. The FROSTSTALKER KING turns to OYSTER, CHIP, and PIC as they huddle beneath a shattered monument.

OYSTER: We've got one shot at this.

CHIP: Piercers hunt in swarms. But if we separate them—

OYSTER: —they fall apart.

Pic squeaks and spreads his wings wide, mimicking a Piercer scattering.

They all glance toward the King. He listens, nodding slowly.

OYSTER: Tell your Froststalkers… solo combat. Split them up. One-on-one.

FROSTSTALKER KING (growling with resolve): We'll divide their blood.

CHIP: That's step one. Step two…

OYSTER: The four of us — me, Chip, Pic, and you — we take the Acidics. Keep them occupied. Distract them.

FROSTSTALKER KING (pausing): Until?

OYSTER: Until the Piercers are down. Then we run.

CHIP: We're not strong enough to win that fight — not yet.

The King looks toward his people. Then the horizon. Then nods firmly.

FROSTSTALKER KING: We live to fight smart. Not die proud.

He raises a frost-etched trident and howls.

FROSTSTALKER KING (roaring): SPLIT THE SKY! ONE FANG — ONE FOE!

His warriors let out a chorus of deep, chilling howls. Then, with incredible speed, they scatter like shards of light — each targeting a different Piercer.

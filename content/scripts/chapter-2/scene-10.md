The three swim out of the crevice into a huge, eerily abandoned kingdom. Towers half-buried in the seabed, coral-covered domes, streets lined with empty shells and bones.

CHIP: No one's been here in years…

Pic squeaks.

They move slowly toward the center. As they approach a ruined throne, a golden fish with a crooked crown suddenly rockets from behind the throne, slamming right into OYSTER's face.

EMERGENT KING (FISH FORM): YOU!!!

The fish screams with raw fury, fins flared and eyes wide with madness.

EMERGENT KING: YOU RUINED EVERYTHING!!

OYSTER (stumbling back): What?! Who even—?

The fish begins to grow, bones cracking, tendrils sprouting — beginning a grotesque evolution back into his massive form.

OYSTER: Wait… wait, I know that face.

He steps back.

OYSTER: You're the Emergent King.

EMERGENT KING: Where are my troops?! Where is my kingdom?!

OYSTER: They're gone. Extinct. We heard what happened…

EMERGENT KING: You heard lies! Lies! They all died… because of you. Because they believed I was dead after the Bloom… self-destructed inside my body!

He roars, his body swelling violently, spines forming down his back. The floor cracks beneath the pressure. Pic shrieks in fear, hiding behind CHIP.

CHIP: We need to get out of here. Now.

The three sprint toward the crevice, the water now turning into a vortex. Behind them, the King's mouth opens unnaturally wide, swallowing all water around it, forming a suction tunnel of doom.

OYSTER: Swim! Swim!

They claw toward the exit — so close. Just inches away from escape. But — OYSTER's foot catches a jagged shard of coral. He trips, spinning in the current. CHIP turns back, trying to grab him—

OYSTER: No—!

With one push, OYSTER slams into CHIP and PIC, pushing them forward — but the suction grabs all three of them, pulling them straight into the mouth of the Emergent King.

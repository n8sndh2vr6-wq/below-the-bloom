OYSTER and CHIP emerge from the castle, swimming into the vast deep. The Purple Paradise glows in the distance, eerie yet familiar.

CHIP: It's still here… untouched.

OYSTER: Feels different, though. Like it's watching.

They share a tense moment before nodding to each other.

OYSTER (CONT'D): Let's get out of here. I need fresh air for the first time in two years.

CHIP: Yeah… we've earned that.

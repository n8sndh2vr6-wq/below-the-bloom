---
title: A Loose Stone
scene: INT. DEEP SEA CREVICE – NIGHT
act: emergent
number: 9
summary: A rest stop turns up a hidden tunnel when Oyster's elbow finds a stone that isn't a stone.
---

The water is calm, the space tight and echoing with gentle bubbles. OYSTER, CHIP, and PIC squeeze into a narrow crevice carved into the rock. They stop to rest.

CHIP: We've been swimming for hours. I can't feel my arms. Do we even have arms anymore?

OYSTER: Let's just stop here a second.

PIC lands gently between them, chirping once.

OYSTER: You tired too, little guy?

Pic squeaks softly.

CHIP (leans back against the rock): You ever think about what we're actually doing here? I mean… we're chasing the words of a liar, of a monster who betrayed everyone.

OYSTER: I know. But… if those powers are real… they're the only thing that can stop him.

CHIP: You think Squid really made all this? All the species, the food chain?

OYSTER: No. I think he told himself he did. So he could feel like he mattered. That's why he wants Earth. A fresh canvas.

Pic makes a small chattering noise, looking between them.

OYSTER: You agree, huh?

Pic squawks.

CHIP (laughs): He's smarter than me, I swear.

A pause. Silence. OYSTER leans back, then suddenly — his elbow presses a loose stone, which grinds aside, revealing a faint tunnel behind it. A soft, eerie blue glow pours through.

OYSTER: What the…?

CHIP: You've gotta be kidding me.

summary: A Piercer ambush nearly costs them everything — until a wounded baby Piercer refuses to fight.

The water is hazy blue, glowing softly with refracted sunlight. OYSTER and CHIP descend into the zone where the Emergent Kingdom once stood, only to find a vast expanse now buried under thick, shifting sand.

CHIP (low, awed): It's all gone…

OYSTER: Swallowed by time or something worse.

They drift forward in silence. In the far distance, across the sandy kingdom, a shadowy figure stands completely still.

CHIP: There it is again…

They swim towards it, tension building with each stroke. The figure doesn't move.

Suddenly, the ground quakes, and currents whirl violently. Both are thrown off balance.

OYSTER (gritting his teeth): It's a trap!

The sand explodes upward, revealing a swarm of PIERCERS bursting out with ear-shattering screeches. Each the size of a car, they hover on insect-like wings, beaks flashing, white fur blurring in motion.

CHIP: Piercers! I thought they were extinct!

OYSTER: They were supposed to be mythical. First agent training — escape vertically!

OYSTER and CHIP shoot upwards, dodging bone-exposed, charging Piercers. The creatures open their chest cages, glowing faintly with internal organs and blades.

CHIP opens his compact toolbox, rapidly tossing gear to OYSTER.

CHIP: Catch!

OYSTER grabs a sleek, glowing katana. He whips it around in tight arcs, slicing through Piercers that get too close.

OYSTER: Come on!

After a flurry of attacks and evasions, the Piercers suddenly retreat, but not before one lets out a deafening screech, causing OYSTER to grab his left ear in pain.

OYSTER (grimacing): Left ear… gone for now.

As the sand settles, OYSTER and CHIP spot one Piercer still hovering, twitching weakly. It's smaller. Its fur is matted. It doesn't have the sharp aggression of the others. It doesn't attack.

CHIP: Hold on… this one didn't fight.

OYSTER: Too small. It's a baby.

The baby Piercer tries to return to the sand — but fails. It shudders and squeaks, terrified.

CHIP reaches into his pack and pulls out a small pouch of fish, tossing a few forward. The Piercer sniffs, then gently devours the food. It lets out a small, cute screech, and begins following them, shyly.

CHIP: We've got a fan.

They swim toward where the figure once stood. It reappears — briefly — before darting downward into the abyss again.

OYSTER: Let's keep going.

The baby Piercer stays close behind, curious and gentle, as the three disappear deeper into the ocean.

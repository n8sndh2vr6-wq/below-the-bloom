title: The Froststalkers Remember
scene: EXT. DEEP OCEAN – CREVICE EXIT – NIGHT
act: reunion
place: icy-trenches

summary: The Froststalker kingdom is alive after all, and Pic — terrified at first — wins the King over.
---

OYSTER, CHIP, and PIC burst out from the narrow crevice, gasping and soaked. As they swim forward, a faint shadowy figure darts around the bend of a massive rock wall.

CHIP: There! You see that?

OYSTER: Yeah. Let's move.

They dart after it, weaving between coral pillars and jagged rock. As they round the bend — their eyes widen.

OYSTER: No way…

CHIP: That's… that's the Froststalker kingdom.

A vast, glowing kingdom stretches out before them, bioluminescent towers carved into the sides of deep trenches, glowing orbs dancing through the current. In the distance — Froststalkers.

OYSTER and CHIP immediately start swimming over. PIC follows, but as soon as he spots the Froststalkers — he freezes. He lets out a tiny, terrified squeak and backs away, pressing close to a rock.

CHIP: Hey — hey, Pic… it's alright. They're not like before. They helped us.

OYSTER reaches out gently, and PIC creeps forward again, hesitantly swimming between them, his body low and tense.

They approach the Froststalkers, who eye them with amused familiarity. A few knock their heads against OYSTER and CHIP's arms as they swim past, playful but a bit rough.

One Froststalker pauses. Its gaze shifts — locking directly onto PIC. It lets out a piercing squawk, spreading its claws slightly, trying to intimidate. But instead of retreating, PIC squeaks louder and clings tighter to OYSTER.

Another Froststalker glides up, sniffing PIC closely.

CHIP: Easy…

CHIP swiftly pulls a mini oxygen gun and fires a small burst — not enough to hurt, just a warning puff. The Froststalker startles, then laughs in a low rumble and swims off excitedly.

The others seem to recognize the trio and circle around, swimming joyfully, squawking in celebration.

OYSTER: They remember us.

A giant shadow moves beneath them. From a cavern below, the FROSTSTALKER KING rises, regal and massive.

PIC hides deeper behind OYSTER. OYSTER turns, lifts him gently into his arms, and swims forward.

OYSTER: We made a new friend.

The King lowers his head. He looks deep into PIC's eyes. PIC squeaks nervously — but this time, not out of fear.

FROSTSTALKER KING: He is… a lovely companion.

PIC, slowly warming, presses his head against the King's giant claw in appreciation. The King hums lowly, accepting the gesture.

CHIP: We all needed this.

The Froststalkers lead them toward a nearby series of ancient stone caves carved into the ridge. Quiet. Safe. The trio enters, bodies exhausted, minds heavy. They settle down — finally, for the first time in what feels like forever, they sleep.

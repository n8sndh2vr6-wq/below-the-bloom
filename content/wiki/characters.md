# Characters

## Oyster

**Age 16 · Human · Survivor**

Lanky, in a pressure suit worn unzipped at the collar until the day it has to
save his life. He is on the expedition because he read the classified files
nobody was supposed to read, and because the planet's numbers don't fit the
rules and that bothers him more than it bothers the adults.

He is not a soldier. Everything he wins in Chapter One he wins badly — with a
suit knife, an oxygen blaster, a fin torn off a corpse, and a refusal to stop
that reads as courage from the outside and stubbornness from the inside.

- **Loses** his right hand to the Maw King in [Scene 21](#/script/chapter-1/scene-21).
- **Gains** a pressure-sealed prosthetic from Chip in [Scene 24](#/script/chapter-1/scene-24).
- **Refuses** the Deadwake Queen's bargain out loud, in front of the kings he was
  told to betray, in [Scene 32](#/script/chapter-1/scene-32).

> **OYSTER:** We're not turning back.

## Chip

**AI companion · Shoulder-mounted projector unit**

Boots up with a flicker, sarcastic by design — *"I was designed to match my
operator's attitude. You set the bar low."* He runs diagnostics, chemistry
readouts, sonar, threat analysis and a running commentary nobody asked for.

The joke keeps landing right up until it doesn't. Chip dives into a horde of
Maws to drop Oyster a weapon in [Scene 23](#/script/chapter-1/scene-23),
transforms into a toolbox to build him a hand in
[Scene 24](#/script/chapter-1/scene-24), and reconfigures into a mounted
blaster for the last fight in [Scene 45](#/script/chapter-1/scene-45).

> **CHIP:** And without you, I wouldn't have existed past that shipwreck.

## Dr. Lena Voss

**30s · Expedition lead · Calm, assertive**

Runs the descent and wants the crew loose and laughing before it. Believes the
structures under the ice mean intelligence, and that intelligence means answers
about *the ones who came before us*. Initiates crash protocol when the surge
hits. Does not survive the impact.

## Tech Officer Mariq Levi

**20s · Jokester · Optimist**

Alien sushi, mutant krakens, evil algae — the crew's morale, right up to the
moment he says *it's like something's already watching us* and the lights
flicker. Does not survive the impact.

## The Froststalker King

**Ruler of the icy trenches**

Massive, armoured, wreathed in icy mist, luminous scars pulsing faint blue. He
arrives roaring for his children and finds them alive, guarded by two strangers
and a dead Maw.

> **KING FROSTSTALKER:** Kindness from above… not for conquest. But to protect.
> That is rare. You are no longer prisoners here.

The only crowned figure still standing at the end of Chapter One. He carries
Oyster on his back into the final charge and walks away into the dark with a
line that sits somewhere between blessing and warning: *Take care of the light,
surface-dwellers.*

## The Maw King

**Ruler of the cavernous depths**

Throne of black coral, orange eyes, mandibles, fluent English delivered low and
gravelled. He is not stupid and not mindless — he is entertained. He takes
Oyster's hand slowly because slowness is the point, then tells him to run
because the running is better theatre than the kill.

Later, in the Bloom's arena, he fights beside Oyster. The Bloom takes control
of him, uses him to cut the Deadwake Queen in half, and then eats him. His
ghost apologises for what he was.

> **MAW KING (spirit):** And defied the monster I once was.

## The Emergent King

**Ruler of the lighted depths · Crown of coral and light**

Melodic, echoing, faintly smug. The Emergents are the oldest beings on the
planet and he speaks like something that has watched a great deal and been
surprised by very little — which makes his respect for two shipwrecked
survivors worth something.

He and the Froststalker King ended a war between their peoples and now prepare
together each year for the Maw hunger. He dies swallowing the Bloom so that it
detonates inside him.

## The Deadwake Queen

**Ruler of the purple paradise · Throne of obsidian coral**

Sleek, enormous, regal, her fins pulsing dominance. Her voice is *smooth, regal,
but sharp like a blade.* She spares Oyster and Chip because they are useful,
then tries to buy them: guide five royal guards into the Emergent kingdom and
guide them to feast, or don't leave alive.

> **DEADWAKE QUEEN:** Do not confuse sentiment with survival.

Betrayed, she brings war. Conscripted, she fights beside them anyway. Killed by
a puppeted Maw King, she goes out grinning: *You'll need more… than that…*

## The Bloom

**The tide itself · Antagonist of Chapter One**

Appears as a small, sleek, glowing fish whose scales ripple like thoughts. It
speaks without moving its mouth, freezes and unfreezes a battlefield of
thousands, and possesses the dead with a ripple of its fins.

> **THE BLOOM:** I am the current, the tide, the teeth, the whisper between each
> breath in the dark. I do not command this world. I am this world.

Its philosophy is the food chain, and its entertainment is watching hope
stretch thin. It ran the crash, the trail, the war and the arena as one
continuous game — and it locked the actual god of this world in a castle to do
it.

## Squid

**The god of this world · Castle throne room**

The body of a man, the head of a squid, tendrils moving calmly in the water. He
made the kings, the Bloom, the tides and the food chain. He was imprisoned in
his own castle by his own creation, and Oyster and Chip's arrival frees him.

He thanks them. He promises to revive the royals and rebuild the peace. Then he
takes their shapes, their ship, their names and their lives, and leaves them on
the throne in the dark.

> **SQUID:** The world is changing. But the throne never does.

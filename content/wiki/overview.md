# Planet (Redacted)

An ocean world. No landmass anyone has recorded, no shoreline, no rules that
hold. From orbit it reads as another ice moon — rolling cloud, auroras, a
surface of endless shifting water. The agency tracked it for years and filed it
as *another Europa knockoff*.

The readings never agreed with that.

> **CHIP:** Confirmed. Planetary core anomalies. Heat signature inconsistencies.
> Subterranean structures — unknown origin.

## The Bloom event

A pulse of purple light expands out of the surface like a ripple through space
itself. It reads as an electromagnetic surge; it behaves like an intention. It
struck the research vessel in orbit, killed its systems in seconds and dragged
it down into the water.

The same violet glow appears at every depth after that — in the wreck's
viewport, along the trench walls, in the corridors of the Maw kingdom, in a
thread of light that leads down and down. It is not weather and it is not an
anomaly. It is a *someone*, and it has been steering the whole way.

## The shape of the deep

The ocean is layered, and each layer belongs to something.

| Layer | Belongs to |
| --- | --- |
| Shallow reef | Bioluminescent algae, alien fish, nothing that bites yet |
| Pitch black zone | Froststalkers, hunting |
| The icy trenches | The Froststalker kingdom |
| Cavernous depths | Maw territory |
| The lighted area | The Emergents |
| Purple paradise | The Deadwakes, waiting |
| The purple abyss | The Bloom |
| Below all of it | The castle |

The Bloom describes this arrangement as scripture rather than ecology:

> The Emergent rises. The Maw consumes. The Frost stalks. The Deadwake waits.
> And I… I decide.

## The castle and the crown

Buried under ten layers of classified nonsense in the agency's files is a
*deep structure* — old, possibly alive, known only from blurry scans taken by
people who then went missing. The legend attached to it says a king lives there
who can send you home.

That legend is the reason Oyster walks out of a survivable wreck into an alien
abyss. Chapter One is the trip down to find out how much of it was true.

*Answer: the castle is real, the king is real, and the legend left out what he
wants.*

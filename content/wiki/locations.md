# Locations

The chapter is a straight line downward. Each location is deeper, darker and
older than the one above it.

## Orbit — the research vessel

Dim blue interior lighting, humming instrument panels, a window full of icy
planet. Warm and ordinary and gone within a page.

→ [Scene 1](#/script/chapter-1/scene-01)

## The wreck

Flickering lights, alarms humming faintly, water dripping from above, low
gravity. Most of the biosignatures gone or too faint to matter. Through a
cracked viewport: endless water and a purple pulse a long way off.

Oyster leaves it with a knife, an oxygen blaster, a scanner and Chip.

→ [Scene 2](#/script/chapter-1/scene-02)

## The shallow reef

Coral-like structures, bioluminescent algae pulsing in the current, alien fish,
beams of light patterning the sand. The only genuinely safe place in the
chapter — and it ends at a cliff edge where the seafloor drops into a trench
*like a mouth*, and every instrument goes flat.

→ [Scene 3](#/script/chapter-1/scene-03)

## The pitch black zone

No light, no readings, no bottom. Froststalker hunting ground. Where Oyster
learns what he is going to have to become.

→ [Scenes 4–5](#/script/chapter-1/scene-04)

## The icy trenches

Jagged cliffs, crystalline frost formations, ancient bones embedded in the
walls, blue lights shimmering like stars caught in glacier ice. Twisting ice
bridges and glowing vents. Nests in the alcoves.

The Froststalker kingdom's caverns run beneath it — holding caves, cave prisons,
a deep cavern chamber that turns out to be a nursery.

→ [Scenes 5–15](#/script/chapter-1/scene-05)

## Maw territory

The descent past the trenches goes murky grey, then dark, then absolute. Sound
is muted by the depth. Half an hour of falling and still no bottom.

Below: cavernous depths, twilight corridors lit sick purple, twisting tunnels
and dead ends. At the centre, a vast hall with a throne of black, spiked coral.

→ [Scenes 16–25](#/script/chapter-1/scene-25)

## The lighted area

Natural light from *below* — the first time in days they can switch their lamps
off. Open water, glowing fish, and one of them is not a fish.

→ [Scene 27](#/script/chapter-1/scene-27)

## The Emergent kingdom

An expansive glowing passage opens into a clearing where structures of coral,
ice and salvaged metal rise like a cityscape. An aquatic palace lit
bioluminescent-warm, shared peacefully by Emergents and visiting Froststalkers.

The site of the war in [Scenes 32–35](#/script/chapter-1/scene-32).

→ [Scene 28](#/script/chapter-1/scene-28)

## Purple paradise

Tall twisting coral spires glowing serene lavender. Trees of translucent
mineral. Schools of bioluminescent fish. Water that is calm, and warm, and
holding its breath.

It is a trap with a view.

→ [Scenes 29–30](#/script/chapter-1/scene-29)

## The Deadwake kingdom

Reached through a shadowed trench at the edge of the paradise. A cathedral-like
expanse with bioluminescent bones set into the walls, and a throne chiselled
from obsidian coral.

→ [Scene 31](#/script/chapter-1/scene-31)

## The purple land / the purple abyss

A deep glowing chamber of shimmering coral, glowing plants and misty purple
light, with a hum in it like a heartbeat. Time works differently here — the
Bloom stops the battle above and restarts it with a ripple.

Above it, in open water, the Bloom builds its arena and runs its game.

→ [Scenes 36–45](#/script/chapter-1/scene-36)

## The castle

The *deep structure* from the agency files. It rises out of the abyss in a
purple hue, glowing faintly, and its gates open by themselves.

**The hall of statues** — the Froststalker King, strong and proud; the Maw King,
fierce and cunning; the Emergent King, elegant and imposing; the Deadwake Queen,
wings spread, eternally mid-flight. And at the end, small and serene and
terrifying, the Bloom.

**The throne room** — a glowing throne carved from obsidian coral, four hulking
guards in the shadows, and a god who has been waiting a very long time for
someone to open the door.

→ [Scenes 46–48](#/script/chapter-1/scene-48)

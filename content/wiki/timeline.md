# Chapter One — Timeline

Forty-eight scenes, one continuous descent.

## I · Descent

1. **Orbit.** The crew jokes about alien sushi. Voss wants one expedition that
   goes right. Mariq says it feels like something is already watching.
   → [Scene 1](#/script/chapter-1/scene-01)
2. **The Bloom hits.** A violet pulse expands off the surface. Five seconds of
   warning, then white light, then water.
3. **The wreck.** Oyster wakes. The crew are gone. He chooses the castle over
   the wreck and swims out of the airlock into the abyss.
   → [Scene 2](#/script/chapter-1/scene-02)
4. **The reef.** Something beautiful, briefly. Then the trench edge, and the
   readings flatline. → [Scene 3](#/script/chapter-1/scene-03)

## II · The Icy Trenches

5. **First blood.** A berserker Froststalker in total darkness. Oyster wins and
   doesn't understand how. → [Scene 4](#/script/chapter-1/scene-04)
6. **The pack.** Dozens of Froststalkers move past in formation, hunting
   something that isn't them. *Something's got them scared.*
   → [Scene 5](#/script/chapter-1/scene-05)
7. **Two sentries.** Oyster tears a fin off a corpse and finishes the fight with
   it. → [Scene 6](#/script/chapter-1/scene-06)
8. **Captured.** Taken into the caves, disarmed, held.
   → [Scenes 7–11](#/script/chapter-1/scene-07)
9. **The nursery.** A Maw is loose among the Froststalker cubs. Oyster kills it
   and guards the babies instead of running.
   → [Scene 11](#/script/chapter-1/scene-11)
10. **The King's judgement.** He arrives roaring and finds his children alive.
    *You are no longer prisoners here.* → [Scene 12](#/script/chapter-1/scene-12)
11. **The kingdom.** Salvage crews, nests, cubs. Every assumption dies quietly.
    → [Scene 14](#/script/chapter-1/scene-14)
12. **Departure.** They leave the only safe place on the planet on purpose.
    → [Scene 15](#/script/chapter-1/scene-15)

## III · Maw Territory

13. **The long fall.** Thirty-six minutes and still no bottom.
    → [Scene 16](#/script/chapter-1/scene-16)
14. **The ambush.** An EM spike kills Chip's systems. Two orange circles open in
    the void. They are driven apart. → [Scene 17](#/script/chapter-1/scene-17)
15. **The throne of black coral.** The Maw King, fluent and unhurried, offers
    Oyster a choice of deaths. → [Scene 20](#/script/chapter-1/scene-20)
16. **The hand.** Taken slowly, on purpose. Oyster kills two guards on rage
    alone and is let go, because running is better sport.
    → [Scene 21](#/script/chapter-1/scene-21)
17. **The dead end.** Cornered — and a blaster clatters to his feet from
    somewhere above. → [Scene 23](#/script/chapter-1/scene-23)
18. **The new hand.** Chip opens like a toolbox. *Welcome back, Two-Handed
    Terror.* → [Scene 24](#/script/chapter-1/scene-24)

## IV · Alliance & War

19. **The lighted area.** Sunlight from below. Oyster stabs dinner. Dinner is an
    Emergent. → [Scene 27](#/script/chapter-1/scene-27)
20. **Two crowns.** The Froststalker and Emergent kings, allied against the Maw
    hunger, warn them about what lives below even them.
    → [Scene 28](#/script/chapter-1/scene-28)
21. **Purple paradise.** The prettiest water in the ocean. A Deadwake opens
    Oyster's chest; its poison calls a dozen more.
    → [Scene 29](#/script/chapter-1/scene-29)
22. **The Queen's price.** Lure five royal guards into the Emergent kingdom, or
    don't leave. They say yes to survive.
    → [Scene 31](#/script/chapter-1/scene-31)
23. **The betrayal, reversed.** Oyster warns the kings instead. The Queen
    declares war. → [Scene 32](#/script/chapter-1/scene-32)
24. **The war.** Frost, Emergent and Deadwake tear each other apart in open
    water. → [Scenes 33–35](#/script/chapter-1/scene-33)

## V · The Bloom's Game

25. **Time stops.** The battle freezes mid-strike. Oyster and Chip are pulled
    down into the purple land. → [Scene 36](#/script/chapter-1/scene-36)
26. **The sermon.** *I do not command this world. I am this world.* The Bloom
    explains the food chain as design, not accident.
    → [Scene 37](#/script/chapter-1/scene-37)
27. **The rules.** Beat a creature and it joins you. It gets worse as it goes.
    The kings come last. → [Scene 38](#/script/chapter-1/scene-38)
28. **The waves.** Froststalkers, Maws, Deadwakes. Their side grows. Some of it
    dies. → [Scene 39](#/script/chapter-1/scene-39)
29. **Four crowns.** The Froststalker King, the Maw King, the Emergent King and
    the Deadwake Queen, one after another.
    → [Scenes 40–43](#/script/chapter-1/scene-40)
30. **The last fight.** Clones and violet blades. The Bloom puppets the Maw
    King, kills the Deadwake Queen with him, then eats him.
    → [Scene 45](#/script/chapter-1/scene-45)
31. **The detonation.** Oyster rides the Froststalker King; Chip becomes the
    cannon. The Bloom is fired into the Emergent King's mouth and
    self-destructs. Three crowns die with it.
32. **The spirits.** *And now… you are what remains of the five.* The
    Froststalker King walks into the dark. *Take care of the light,
    surface-dwellers.*

## VI · The Castle

33. **The gate.** It opens for them. Neither of them speaks for a while.
    → [Scene 46](#/script/chapter-1/scene-46)
34. **The hall.** Statues of everyone they fought. *Conflict does not mean
    emptiness. It means we shared time with giants.*
    → [Scene 47](#/script/chapter-1/scene-47)
35. **The throne room.** Squid — the god who made all of it and was locked in
    here by the Bloom. He thanks them.
    → [Scene 48](#/script/chapter-1/scene-48)
36. **The theft.** He becomes Oyster. A second Chip materialises. *I will go to
    your Earth. Be your heroes. Take your ship. Take your names.*
37. **The throne.** Four guards. Doors sealed. Purple light. Two voices walking
    away that are not theirs.

> **FADE TO BLACK.**

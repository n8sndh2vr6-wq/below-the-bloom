# The Five Powers

Four kingdoms hold the ocean between them. A fifth thing holds the four.

> The Emergent rises. The Maw consumes. The Frost stalks. The Deadwake waits.
> And I… I decide.
>
> — **The Bloom**, [Scene 37](#/script/chapter-1/scene-37)

---

## ❄️ The Froststalkers

**Territory:** the icy trenches · **Ruler:** the Froststalker King

Fast, armoured predators with ice-hardened cartilage in their fins — sharp
enough to be torn free and swung as a blade, which is exactly what Oyster does
with one in [Scene 6](#/script/chapter-1/scene-06).

There is a **berserker class** that hunts alone and a common class that hunts in
coordinated packs. Both are terrifying in the black zone. Neither is the whole
story.

Inside the trenches they are a *society*: nests built into icy alcoves, work
crews hauling salvaged hull plating across frozen bridges, adults nudging cubs
along and growling encouragement. Bioluminescent crystals light the walls.
Ancient bones sit embedded in the ice.

> **CHIP:** It's almost… civil. Like they've been building this for a long time.
> A society hidden in the deep.

They are the only kingdom left standing at the end of Chapter One.

## 🦑 The Maws

**Territory:** the cavernous depths · **Ruler:** the Maw King

Orange eyes in total darkness, mandibles, jagged teeth, claws that click on
coral. They ambush in the black and they jam electronics — an electromagnetic
spike is what separates Oyster from Chip in
[Scene 17](#/script/chapter-1/scene-17).

The Maws are the kingdom the other kingdoms organise against. The Froststalkers
and Emergents prepare together every year, *when the currents shift and the Maw
hunger grows*, because it is the only way either survives.

Their king is the cruellest figure in the chapter and the only one who is
cruel on purpose. He is also, at the very end, sorry.

## 🐋 The Emergents

**Territory:** the lighted area below Maw country · **Ruler:** the Emergent King

The oldest beings on the planet, and the most dangerous thing to underestimate.
An Emergent can hold the shape of a small glowing fish indefinitely — until
something disturbs it, at which point limbs extend, plates form, scales harden,
and it grows into a shark-dragon the size of the iceberg that sank the Titanic.

Oyster stabs one because he is hungry. It was a baby.

> **CHIP:** Congratulations. You've managed to stab what might be the oldest
> being on this planet.

Their kingdom is a cityscape of coral, ice and salvaged metal, lit warm and
shared peacefully with visiting Froststalkers.

## 🌑 The Deadwakes

**Territory:** the purple paradise · **Ruler:** the Deadwake Queen

Long, eel-like, armoured, streaked with bioluminescence. They screech like
ripping metal, they read your movements and dodge before you commit, and their
blood is a purple poison that does not disperse — it *calls*. Kill one and a
dozen arrive.

Their kingdom sits behind the most beautiful water in the ocean, a
cathedral-like expanse walled in bioluminescent bone. Beautiful and horrifying,
in that order.

The Deadwakes are the faction that offers Oyster a real moral choice rather than
a fight, which is what makes them the turning point of the chapter.

## 🟣 The Bloom

**Territory:** all of it · **Ruler:** itself

Not a kingdom. A fifth power that sits over the other four and calls the
arrangement *balance*.

It presents as a small glowing fish and behaves as weather, prophecy and
puppeteer. It halts a battlefield mid-strike, restarts it at will, raises the
fallen with purple in their eyes, splits into hundreds of blade-wielding
clones, and describes all of it as entertainment.

> **THE BLOOM:** Power is not control. It is belief. When all eyes turn to you
> for answers, when the ocean holds its breath at your presence… that is power.

It is also a usurper. According to [Squid](#/wiki/characters), the Bloom was
*made* — and then locked its maker in a castle and took the tide for itself.

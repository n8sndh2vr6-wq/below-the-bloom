# Gear & Technology

Everything Oyster owns came off a dead ship, and most of it was designed for
something other than what he uses it for.

## Pressure suit

Standard expedition wear, worn unzipped at the collar until the crash. It
auto-seals minor tears with a hiss, runs helmet speakers that distort a scream
into something worse, and carries a shoulder projector port for Chip.

It is not armour. Every fight in the chapter leaves marks on it.

## Oxygen pressure blaster

A tool, not a weapon — until it is. It fires a concentrated stream of compressed
oxygen and does three completely different jobs over the chapter:

- **Point-blank wound.** Fired into an open jaw in [Scene 6](#/script/chapter-1/scene-06).
- **Thruster.** Aimed *downward* to escape an Emergent's vortex in [Scene 27](#/script/chapter-1/scene-27).
- **Kill shot.** Punched clean through the Maw King's cheek in [Scene 23](#/script/chapter-1/scene-23).

> **CHIP:** He skewered a Maw with a high-pressure oxygen stream. You should've
> seen it. Messy. Effective.

## Suit knife

Small, sharp, and responsible for most of Oyster's problems — it's the knife
that cuts the glowing fish that turns out to be a baby Emergent, and the knife
that cuts the fish that summons a Deadwake. It is also what he reaches for
first, every time.

## Froststalker fin

Improvised. Torn from a corpse mid-fight in
[Scene 6](#/script/chapter-1/scene-06), jagged and edged with ice-hardened
cartilage, swung like a blade and then buried in a neck.

> **CHIP:** I'll add "makeshift weapons specialist" to your resume.

## The prosthetic hand

Built by Chip out of a toolbox hard drive salvaged from a crashed sub-pod near
the drop zone. Sleek silvery metal, softly glowing circuitry, shaped to match
Oyster's severed wrist. It magnetises, syncs with a hiss and a pulse, and every
finger responds like it was always there.

**Specifications, per Chip:** pressure-sealed, neural-link capable, bottle
opener.

→ [Scene 24](#/script/chapter-1/scene-24)

# Chip

Chip is gear and he is also a person, which is most of the point of him.

## Forms

| Form | Purpose | First seen |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Floating companion | Diagnostics, light, sonar, commentary | [Scene 1](#/script/chapter-1/scene-01) |
| Helmet projector | Displays readouts on Oyster's visor | [Scene 2](#/script/chapter-1/scene-02) |
| Toolbox | Opens like a transformer; internal compartments | [Scene 24](#/script/chapter-1/scene-24) |
| Battering ram | Full-speed body slam into a Deadwake | [Scene 29](#/script/chapter-1/scene-29) |
| Mounted blaster | Cannon form, ridden into the final charge | [Scene 45](#/script/chapter-1/scene-45) |

## Capabilities

- Chemical and biological analysis, projected as holograms
- Biosignature scanning — how he knows the crew is gone
- Sonar pinging
- Threat targeting: *"They're illusions! Scattering signals — wait… that one!
  Eleven degrees northeast!"*
- Escape-route calculation, sixty-seven plans in ten seconds, all of them fatal
- A downloaded philosophy pack he did not mention acquiring

## Vulnerabilities

Electromagnetic interference. A spike on the descent into Maw territory
glitches his hologram, cuts his voice and jams him away from Oyster for four
scenes — the longest either of them is alone in the chapter.

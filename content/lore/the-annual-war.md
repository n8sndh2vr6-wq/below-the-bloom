Once a year, when the currents shift, the [Maw](#/creatures/maws) hunger grows
and the kingdoms above them have to survive it.

## The alliance

The [Froststalkers](#/creatures/froststalkers) and
[Emergents](#/creatures/emergents) were enemies. They are not any more, and the
reason is arithmetic rather than sentiment.

> **FROSTSTALKER KING:** Yes. Once enemies. Now united. We prepare each year —
> when the currents shift and the Maw hunger grows. It is the only way to survive.

Their kings meet in the [Emergent kingdom](#/locations/emergent-kingdom) to plan
it. Their peoples move through each other's territory without incident. It is
the only functioning diplomacy in Chapter One.

## The year it came early

> **EMERGENT KING:** It will come sooner than expected. The pressure stirs. The
> deep trembles.

He is right, and he is wrong about the cause. The war that arrives is not the
Maws at all — it is the [Deadwake Queen](#/characters/deadwake-queen), who has
her own plans and wanted [Oyster](#/characters/oyster) to open the gates for her.

Oyster warns the kings instead, in front of her, and she brings the war herself.

> **EMERGENT KING:** Then this is WAR!

## What it costs

Froststalkers, Emergents and Deadwakes tear each other apart across the plazas
and out into open water for three scenes. Nobody wins. Then a screech splits the
battlefield, everything stops, and the water begins to glow faint purple.

The war was never really theirs. See [the food chain](#/lore/the-food-chain).

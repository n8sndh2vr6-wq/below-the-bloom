Thirty-six scenes, nine acts. Read the
[full screenplay](#/story) alongside it.

## I · The Return

1. **The doors open.** No one touches them. Oyster and Chip wake to a shadow in
   the corridor. → [Scene 1](#/story/chapter-2/scene-01)
2. **Out through the paradise.** Free, and it still feels watched.
   → [Scene 2](#/story/chapter-2/scene-02)
3. **Air.** Two years under, then the wreck of their own ship by moonlight.
   → [Scene 3](#/story/chapter-2/scene-03)

## II · Whispers of Power

4. **The decision.** Squid's "ancient powers" were mentioned once, in passing.
   Oyster wants to find them first. → [Scene 4](#/story/chapter-2/scene-04)
5. **An empty kingdom.** The Froststalkers are gone, and something is watching
   from the ruins. → [Scene 5](#/story/chapter-2/scene-05)

## III · The Maw King's Debt

6. **The reunion.** Alive, and believed dead. The Maw King gives the tally —
   Emergents extinct, Deadwakes imploded, Froststalkers vanished — and pledges
   to shadow them at a distance. → [Scene 6](#/story/chapter-2/scene-06)

## IV · The Piercers

7. **The ambush.** A watching shadow lures them into a swarm; a wounded baby
   Piercer refuses to fight. → [Scene 7](#/story/chapter-2/scene-07)
8. **Pic.** Named mid-swim. The swarm that raised him tries to take him back;
   Oyster ends it. → [Scene 8](#/story/chapter-2/scene-08)

## V · The Emergent King

9. **The loose stone.** A rest stop opens into a hidden tunnel.
   → [Scene 9](#/story/chapter-2/scene-09)
10. **Revived, and worse.** A second Emergent King, alone and grieving, blames
    them and swallows all three. → [Scene 10](#/story/chapter-2/scene-10)
11. **The throat.** A katana jammed into fleshy wall is the only thing between
    them and a pit of acid. → [Scene 11](#/story/chapter-2/scene-11)
12. **The rocket.** Oyster fires himself back up through the King's own throat.
    → [Scene 12](#/story/chapter-2/scene-12)
13. **Out.** The buried kingdom cracks and sinks behind them, a second time.
    → [Scene 13](#/story/chapter-2/scene-13)

## VI · Froststalker Reunion

14. **They remember.** The kingdom is alive after all; Pic — terrified at first —
    wins the King over. → [Scene 14](#/story/chapter-2/scene-14)
15. **The story, interrupted.** An Acidic finds the cave mid-telling.
    → [Scene 15](#/story/chapter-2/scene-15)

## VII · The Siege of the Trenches

16. **The wasteland.** The kingdom is under siege by Piercers and Acidics
    both. → [Scene 16](#/story/chapter-2/scene-16)
17. **Split the sky.** Scatter the Piercers, stall the Acidics by hand.
    → [Scene 17](#/story/chapter-2/scene-17)
18. **The tide turns.** The plan works, for now.
    → [Scenes 18–19](#/story/chapter-2/scene-18)
19. **The chase.** Acidics in pursuit through the trench; a hiding spot, an
    ambush, and a trap that springs too well.
    → [Scenes 20–23](#/story/chapter-2/scene-20)
20. **Piercers to the rescue.** Cornered, Oyster gambles on the swarm beneath the
    sand, and Pic vouches for them. → [Scene 24](#/story/chapter-2/scene-24)

## VIII · The Piercer Queen

21. **The ultimatum.** Pic's mother finds him; the Froststalker King pays for
    standing between them. → [Scene 26](#/story/chapter-2/scene-26)
22. **The Queen falls.** The Maw King turns the tide; Oyster finishes it from
    inside her own ribcage. → [Scene 27](#/story/chapter-2/scene-27)
23. **The silhouette speaks.** A stranger beheads her corpse, wrecks Chip, and
    vanishes without a name. → [Scene 28](#/story/chapter-2/scene-28)
24. **One more fight.** Chip comes back online for another Acidic wave; Oyster's
    fury finally shows its teeth. → [Scene 29](#/story/chapter-2/scene-29)

## IX · The Monument

25. **Skywalkers.** A stampede of colossal legs nearly finishes what the Acidics
    couldn't. → [Scene 30](#/story/chapter-2/scene-30)
26. **The King's last stand.** The Acidic King corners them at the monument's
    foot. The Froststalker King pays the final price.
    → [Scene 31](#/story/chapter-2/scene-31)
27. **The hallway.** A quiet walk through everything the chapter cost them.
    → [Scene 32](#/story/chapter-2/scene-32)
28. **Mach.** A blade from behind, and the silhouette finally gives his name.
    → [Scene 33](#/story/chapter-2/scene-33)
29. **The duel.** Fire, blades, acid and ice, fought to a standstill at the foot
    of the pedestal. → [Scene 34](#/story/chapter-2/scene-34)
30. **The spire.** Mach slips away alive, and reappears where a king fell.
    → [Scene 35](#/story/chapter-2/scene-35)
31. **The orb.** Oyster's hand closes on it at last. A portal opens.

> **ALL:** Squid… we'll find you.
>
> **CUT TO BLACK.**

A pulse of purple light that expands out of the ocean's surface *like a ripple
through space itself*. It reads on instruments as an electromagnetic surge. It
behaves like an intention.

## The event

The research vessel is minutes from descent, the crew is laughing, and the lights
flicker for half a second. The hum of the ship shifts tone. Everyone freezes for
a beat.

> **MARIQ:** Still gives me the creeps. Like something's… watching us already.

Then the monitor catches it — violet light expanding off the surface, growing
impossibly bright.

> **CHIP:** Warning. Electromagnetic surge inbound. Impact in five… four…

The wave hits. The lights explode into white. Systems spark, sirens shriek, and
the ship is dragged down into a churning violet ocean.

[Dr. Voss](#/characters/lena-voss) and [Mariq Levi](#/characters/mariq-levi) do
not survive the impact.

## It never stops

The same violet glow appears at every depth after the crash.

- Pulsing far off in the water outside the [wreck](#/locations/the-wreck)'s
  cracked viewport.
- Flickering across the [Maw King](#/characters/maw-king)'s spines in his throne
  room.
- Lighting the corridors and dead ends of [Maw territory](#/locations/maw-territory).
- Bleeding out of a dead [Deadwake](#/creatures/deadwakes) as poison that summons
  more.
- Running through the water as a thread that Oyster and Chip follow down, and
  down, and down.

## What it actually was

Not weather. Not an anomaly. A *someone*, steering the whole way.

> **THE BLOOM:** I am the current, the tide, the teeth, the whisper between each
> breath in the dark.

The crash was not an accident the [Bloom](#/characters/the-bloom) took advantage
of. It was the opening move.

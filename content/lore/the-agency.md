The organisation that funded the expedition, tracked
[Planet (Redacted)](#/locations/planet-redacted) for years, and classified almost
everything it found.

## What they knew

Officially: another Europa knockoff. An ice moon with an ocean, worth a survey.

Unofficially, buried under ten layers of classification: core anomalies, heat
signature inconsistencies, subterranean structures of unknown origin, and blurry
scans of a *deep structure* they described as old and possibly alive.

> **OYSTER:** The agency. They were tracking this planet for years. Thought it
> was just another Europa knockoff. But something's here, Chip. Something huge.

## The missing

The files Oyster obsessed over were written by people who went missing. Chip
points this out before they leave the wreck, in as many words:

> **CHIP:** Just to be clear — you're trusting legends you read in half-corrupted
> agency logs written by people who went missing?
>
> **OYSTER:** Yeah. Pretty much.

## What they were hoping for

[Dr. Voss](#/characters/lena-voss) gives the only statement of purpose anyone
offers, and it is not about minerals.

> **DR. VOSS:** If there's intelligence down there… it changes everything. Maybe
> answers about the ones who came before us.

*The ones who came before us* goes unexplained. It is the one thread Chapter One
opens and does not pull.

## The thing about the ending

By the close of the chapter, something wearing Oyster's face and travelling with
a copy of Chip is on its way to Earth with the expedition's ship and the
expedition's names — which means the Agency is about to get its survey back, and
will have no way of knowing what filed it.

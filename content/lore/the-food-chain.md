Four kingdoms hold the ocean between them. A fifth thing holds the four, and
calls the arrangement balance.

> The Emergent rises. The Maw consumes. The Frost stalks. The Deadwake waits.
> And I… I decide.
>
> — [The Bloom](#/characters/the-bloom), [Scene 37](#/story/chapter-1/scene-37)

Depth is rank. Each layer is older and worse than the one above it, and each
kingdom knows exactly what lives beneath it.

> **FROSTSTALKER KING:** If you continue downward, know this — what lives beneath
> even us… is not like the Maws. It is older. And far more deadly.

## The fifth

The Bloom is not a kingdom. It presents as a small glowing fish and sits over
the whole arrangement as weather, prophecy and puppeteer at once — halting a
battlefield mid-strike, restarting it at will, and raising the dead with violet
in their eyes.

Its account of the arrangement is scripture, not ecology. It is not describing a
system it observed. It is describing one it wrote.

> **THE BLOOM:** This world you crash-landed on… it is not chaos. It is not war.
> It is balance. A food chain, perfectly tuned across time. But balance… must be
> maintained.

## The clause underneath

According to [Squid](#/characters/squid), even that is secondhand. He made the
kings, the tides and the food chain — and he made the Bloom, which then took the
arrangement off him and locked him in [the castle](#/locations/the-castle).

Which means the balance the Bloom preaches is a stolen design, defended by the
thief.

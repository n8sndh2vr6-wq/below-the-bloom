Buried under ten layers of classified nonsense in
[the Agency's](#/lore/the-agency) files is a *deep structure* — old, possibly
alive, known only from blurry scans taken by people who then went missing.

Attached to it is a legend: a king lives there who can send you home.

## Why it matters

That legend is the entire reason there is a story. Oyster wakes up in a
survivable [wreck](#/locations/the-wreck) with breathable air and a dead crew,
and instead of waiting for a rescue that is not coming, he walks out of the
airlock into an alien abyss.

> **OYSTER:** The castle. The deep structure. The thing even the agency only had
> blurry scans of. They said it was old, maybe even alive.
>
> **CHIP:** You're suggesting we what — go sightseeing? In an alien trench? With a
> knife and a glorified water gun?

Chip's objection is the correct one. Oyster goes anyway.

> **OYSTER:** I'm suggesting we do the only thing we can. We find it. The king
> they talked about, the one who can send us home… if that's real, that's our only
> way out of this.

## Was it true?

Every part of it.

The [castle](#/locations/the-castle) is real. It is at the bottom. Its gates open
by themselves. There is a king inside, he made this world, and he is entirely
capable of sending someone to Earth.

The legend simply never mentioned what he wanted in exchange, or that *someone*
and *you* were not going to be the same person.

> **SQUID:** I will go to your Earth. Be your heroes. Take your ship. Take your
> names.

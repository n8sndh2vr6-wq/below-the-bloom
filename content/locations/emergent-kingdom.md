Below Maw country the darkness starts to fade and natural light comes up from
*beneath* — the first time in days Oyster and Chip can switch their lamps off.

> **CHIP:** Mark the calendar. First sunlit moment in days. I was starting to
> think the ocean had a personal vendetta against photons.

## The lighted area

Open water, warm light, and schools of glowing fish. It is also where Oyster
stabs one of those fish for dinner and it turns into an
[Emergent](#/creatures/emergents) the size of an iceberg.

## The kingdom

An expansive, softly glowing passage lined with bioluminescent coral opens into
a massive clearing. Structures of coral, ice and salvaged metal rise like a
cityscape. An aquatic palace glows bioluminescent-warm, and
[Froststalkers](#/creatures/froststalkers) swim its halls and plazas alongside
Emergent creatures without incident.

> **CHIP:** That's… that's a kingdom. An actual underwater kingdom.

It is the only place in Chapter One that looks like peace, which is why the
[Deadwake Queen](#/characters/deadwake-queen) wants Oyster to open it up for her
guards, and why the war that follows lands as hard as it does.

## The war

[Scenes 32–35](#/story/chapter-1/scene-32) — Froststalkers, Emergents and
Deadwakes tearing each other apart across the plazas and out into open water,
until the water goes still and something underneath decides it has seen enough.

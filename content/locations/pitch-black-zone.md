No light. No readings. No bottom.

The zone below the [reef](#/locations/shallow-reef) is where the planet stops
being beautiful. The only illumination is the faint glow of Oyster's mask, which
is enough to see a corpse drifting and not enough to see what is coming.

## What lives here

[Froststalkers](#/creatures/froststalkers), hunting. The first one Oyster meets
is a berserker-class that hunts alone and nearly splits him in half — the fight
that turns a stranded teenager into something that can survive down here.

> **OYSTER:** You saw that, right? I actually — we actually did that.
>
> **CHIP:** That was either impressive or incredibly stupid. I haven't decided yet.

## The pack

Later, a rhythmic thumping in the distance, and then dozens of Froststalkers
tear past in coordinated formation. Oyster and Chip hide behind a rock and hold
still.

They are not being hunted. Something else is happening.

> **CHIP:** That… that was a pack. I thought they hunted solo?
>
> **OYSTER:** Something's got them scared.

Past the black zone the terrain gives way to jagged cliffs, crystalline frost
and ancient bones embedded in the walls.

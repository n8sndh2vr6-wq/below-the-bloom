A deep glowing chamber of shimmering coral, glowing plants and misty purple
light, with a low hum in it like a heartbeat. Also called *the purple land*.

Time works differently here. The [Bloom](#/characters/the-bloom) freezes a
battlefield of thousands mid-strike in [Scene 35](#/story/chapter-1/scene-35),
holds them suspended while it talks, and restarts them with a ripple of its
fins.

> **THE BLOOM:** Time resumes.

## What happens in it

This is where the Bloom explains itself — the [food chain](#/lore/the-food-chain)
as design rather than accident, power as belief rather than control, and death
as the only thing that makes life matter.

It is also where the fallen rise with purple in their eyes, awake and not in
control, and where the [Froststalker King](#/characters/froststalker-king)
arrives dragging a spear to shout a warning nobody can act on.

> **FROSTSTALKER KING:** Do not speak to it! That thing — it twists fate itself!

## The arena

Above the chamber, in open water, the Bloom builds its game: wave after wave of
creatures, each defeat adding to Oyster's side, the four crowns held back for
last, and the Bloom itself waiting alone at the centre of the abyss.

> **THE BLOOM:** But let's see how you fare against me. The tide… has always been
> mine.

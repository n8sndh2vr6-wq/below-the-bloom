Tall, twisting coral spires glowing a serene lavender. Trees made of translucent
mineral. Schools of bioluminescent fish drifting through water that is calm, and
warm, and holding its breath.

> **OYSTER:** This place… it's nothing like the rest of the depths.
>
> **CHIP:** No screeching, no glowing eyes. Just… peace. I almost forgot what
> that felt like.

It is a trap with a view.

## What it costs

Oyster cuts a small glowing fish for food. The light dims, a shape passes behind
them with unnatural grace, and a [Deadwake](#/creatures/deadwakes) opens his
chest with a talon.

He kills it. That is worse — the purple poison leaking from the body does not
disperse, it summons. A dozen more arrive and start to circle.

## Why it matters

This is the location where the chapter stops being about survival. Everything up
to here has been teeth. What waits at the far edge of the paradise is a
[queen with an offer](#/locations/deadwake-kingdom), and Oyster's answer to it is
the moral spine of the whole descent.

A vast coral-reef region in early morning light, with bioluminescent plants
pulsing gently in the current and strange alien fish darting past. Soft beams of
light come down from above and paint patterns on the sand.

It is the only genuinely safe water in Chapter One, and it lasts one scene.

> **CHIP:** I have to say, for a planet that just tried to murder us… this is
> kind of stunning.

Chip identifies the glow as bioluminescent algae reacting to salinity. Oyster
prefers *glowing like it's on fire*. They argue about it. It is the last easy
conversation either of them has for a long time.

## The edge

They swim over a coral archway and the terrain changes underneath them. The
ocean floor drops away into utter blackness — a yawning trench stretching out
*like a mouth*.

> **CHIP:** Readings just flatlined. I can't see anything down there.
>
> **OYSTER:** That's our path, isn't it?
>
> **CHIP:** Looks like it. Welcome to the real Planet (Redacted).

Open water breaking into open air. After two years sealed inside
[the castle](#/locations/the-castle), it is the first sky either of them has
seen.

> **CHIP:** A long inhale, the rush of air.

Their old ship still floats nearby, bent and half-sunken, lit silver by the
moon — the same wreck they swam out of at the start of everything.

> **CHIP:** It's still here…
>
> **OYSTER:** Just like us.

## What it isn't

It isn't rest. Oyster and Chip float against the hull long enough to catch their
breath and talk themselves back into the water — Squid's "ancient powers" are
still out there, and neither of them believes the peace will hold if he ever
comes back for them.

> **OYSTER:** That peace? It won't last if Squid ever comes back. We need to be
> ready.

They dive again before the moon has moved very far.

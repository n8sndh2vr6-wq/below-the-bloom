A towering stone structure glowing faintly with ancient runes, standing in a
chamber so deep that colossal [Skywalkers](#/creatures/skywalkers) stampede
across the floor above it without ever finding the entrance.

> **OYSTER:** There — they skid behind a coral-encrusted wall opposite the
> monument, the legs thundering past too numerous to count.

It is what Oyster and Chip came back down for: proof that Squid's "ancient
powers" were never a myth.

## The spire

A central spire rises at the monument's foot, out in the open water before the
doors. It is where the [Froststalker King](#/characters/froststalker-king)
makes his last stand, launching himself and the wounded
[Acidic King](#/characters/acidic-king) onto the point together — a sacrifice
that kills them both and clears the path in.

> **OYSTER:** He… he saved us.

It is also where [Mach](#/characters/mach) reappears afterward, unhurt, watching
from exactly where the King fell.

## The hallway

A corridor lit the same violet as the orb inside. Oyster and Chip walk it slowly
for once, running back through everything the chapter cost them — the
Froststalkers, the Maws, the castle, Squid.

> **OYSTER:** This is it!

## The grand chamber

Stone doors open onto a pedestal holding a single glowing purple orb. It is
where Oyster is stabbed from behind the instant he reaches for it, and where
[Mach](#/characters/mach) finally steps into the light and gives his name.

The duel that follows — Pic's fire, Chip's shield, Oyster's blaster, Mach's
knives and a stolen vial of ice — ends with Oyster's hand closing on the orb at
last. A portal of violet energy opens around the three of them.

> **ALL:** Squid… we'll find you.

Jagged cliffs and crystalline frost formations. Ancient bones set into the walls.
Blue lights shimmering faintly, *like scattered stars caught in glacier ice*.

This is the Froststalker kingdom, and it is the first evidence that the ocean
contains nations rather than only teeth.

## Above ground

Twisting ice bridges, glowing vents, and bioluminescent crystals lining the
cavern walls. [Froststalkers](#/creatures/froststalkers) move through in
organised patterns. Work crews carry pieces of the crashed research vessel
across a frozen path. Others tend nests built into icy alcoves.

> **CHIP:** It's almost… civil. Like they've been building this for a long time.
> A society hidden in the deep.

## Below

The kingdom's caverns run beneath the trenches — holding caves, a cave prison,
and a deep cavern chamber that turns out to be a nursery. Oyster and Chip are
held in one and end up defending the other.

That is where the [King](#/characters/froststalker-king) finds them, and where
prisoners become guests.

## Leaving

They walk out on purpose, past the border, with a warm farewell and no good
reason except the [castle](#/locations/the-castle).

> **CHIP:** Yeah… we've still got a long swim to that castle.

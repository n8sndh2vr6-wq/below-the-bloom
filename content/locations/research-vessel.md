Dim blue interior lighting. Humming instrument panels. A large window showing an
icy planet below — rolling clouds, auroras, endless water.

It is warm, ordinary and crowded with people making jokes, and it exists for
about four pages.

## Aboard

- [Dr. Lena Voss](#/characters/lena-voss), expedition lead, running the descent.
- [Mariq Levi](#/characters/mariq-levi), tech officer, filing complaints about
  alien seafood.
- [Oyster](#/characters/oyster), sixteen, lounging in a chair with his pressure
  suit unzipped at the collar.
- [Chip](#/characters/chip), dormant, projecting soft diagnostic lights until he
  boots up with an insult.

## The last minute

The lights flicker for half a second. The hum of the ship shifts tone. Everyone
freezes for a beat.

Then the monitor shows a pulse of purple light expanding from the planet's
surface, like a ripple through space itself, growing impossibly bright.

> **CHIP:** Warning. Electromagnetic surge inbound. Impact in five… four…

The wave hits. The lights explode into white. The ship spins, sirens shrieking,
and through the front window the glowing violet ocean gets larger and larger
beneath them.

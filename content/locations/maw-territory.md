The descent past the [icy trenches](#/locations/icy-trenches) goes murky grey,
then dark, then absolute. Sound is muted by the crushing depth. Thirty-six
minutes of falling and still no bottom.

> **OYSTER:** Still no bottom. We've been going for — what? Half an hour?

## The country

Cavernous depths opening into twilight corridors lit a sick purple, then
twisting tunnels and dead ends that close behind you. The rock glows faintly
violet where it is touched.

The territory itself is hostile to machines: an electromagnetic spike kills
Oyster's mask light and cuts [Chip](#/characters/chip) off mid-sentence, which is
how the two of them get separated for four scenes.

## The throne room

A vast hall with a throne of black coral, twisted and spiked, at its centre.
[The Maw King](#/characters/maw-king) sits on it with guards hissing behind him
and purple light flickering across his spines.

This is where Oyster loses his right hand, kills two guards with the other one,
and is released for sport.

> **MAW KING:** Splendid. Run, then — if you can.

## What he leaves with

A prosthetic hand built out of a salvaged toolbox hard drive, and the knowledge
that the thing at the bottom of the ocean is not the worst thing in it.

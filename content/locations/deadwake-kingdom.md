Reached through a shadowed trench at the far edge of the
[purple paradise](#/locations/purple-paradise), flanked by two enormous guards.

The trench opens into a cathedral-like expanse. Bioluminescent bones line the
walls. It is beautiful, and horrifying, in that order.

## The throne chamber

A throne chiselled from obsidian coral, guards motionless around it, and
[the Queen](#/characters/deadwake-queen) floating onto it while Oyster and Chip
hang in the water looking very small.

Her terms are delivered without heat:

> **DEADWAKE QUEEN:** There is a war coming. One we do not intend to lose. You
> will lure five of my royal guards into the Emergent kingdom. You will guide them
> to feast. If you do not… you will not leave here alive.

## The answer

Oyster says yes, because there is no version of the conversation where he says
no and lives. Chip has already run the numbers.

> **CHIP:** I have calculated sixty-seven escape plans in the past ten seconds.
> All of them end in death. Just saying.

He leaves with five massive Deadwakes trailing behind him like hungry shadows —
and with a promise he keeps in a way the Queen does not expect.

> **OYSTER:** We'll do it. But when this is over… I'm coming back.

Flickering lights. Alarms humming faintly. Water dripping from above. Low
gravity, so Oyster wakes up floating a few inches off a metal floor with his
suit hissing as it auto-seals a tear.

Most of the biosignatures are gone, or too faint to matter.

> **CHIP:** You got smacked by cosmic weirdness and decided a metal floor was a
> good place to sleep. We crashed. Whole ship. Systems are offline. We're alive.
> Technically. Congratulations.

## Through the viewport

A crack in the glass, endless water beyond it, and a dim purple glow pulsing far
off in the depths.

That glow is the reason he does not stay.

## What he takes

A small oxygen blaster and a scanner out of a locker, his suit knife, and Chip
hooked into the shoulder projector port. Nothing else in the ship is worth
anything.

> **CHIP:** No. I'll stay in the haunted wreck with the corpses. Of course I'm
> coming.

The airlock hisses open onto the freezing alien abyss and they go.

The *deep structure* from the Agency's files — old, possibly alive, known only
from blurry scans taken by people who then went missing.

It rises out of the abyss in a purple hue, glowing faintly. The gates open by
themselves.

> **OYSTER:** We made it…
>
> **CHIP:** I believe this is what humans call… the end of the line.

## The hall of statues

Towering figures line the hall in order. The
[Froststalker King](#/characters/froststalker-king), strong and proud. The
[Maw King](#/characters/maw-king), fierce and cunning. The
[Emergent King](#/characters/emergent-king), elegant and imposing. The
[Deadwake Queen](#/characters/deadwake-queen), wings spread wide, eternally
mid-flight.

And at the end of the row, the [Bloom](#/characters/the-bloom). Small. Serene.
Terrifying.

> **OYSTER:** We fought them all… and somehow, I miss some of them.
>
> **CHIP:** Conflict does not mean emptiness. It means we shared time with giants.

## The throne room

A sprawling room and a glowing throne carved from obsidian coral, with
[Squid](#/characters/squid) sitting on the steps waiting for someone to open the
door.

The legend Oyster chased said there was a king here who could send him home. The
legend was true. It just left out what he wanted in exchange.

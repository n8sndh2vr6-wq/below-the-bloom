An ocean world. No landmass anyone has recorded, no shoreline, and no rules that
hold.

From orbit it reads as another ice moon — rolling cloud, shimmering auroras, and
a surface of endless, shifting water. [The Agency](#/lore/the-agency) tracked it
for years and filed it as another Europa knockoff.

The readings never agreed with that filing.

> **CHIP:** Confirmed. Planetary core anomalies. Heat signature inconsistencies.
> Subterranean structures — unknown origin.

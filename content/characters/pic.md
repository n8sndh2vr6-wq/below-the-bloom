Small, matted-furred, and found twitching in the sand after the swarm that
almost kills Oyster and Chip in [Scene 7](#/story/chapter-2/scene-07). It
doesn't attack. It can't even get back into the ground it came from.

> **CHIP:** Hold on… this one didn't fight.
>
> **OYSTER:** Too small. It's a baby.

Chip feeds it. It follows them, shyly, and does not stop.

## The naming

A few scenes later, still just "it," Oyster runs through names while it hovers
between him and Chip, listening to every word.

> **OYSTER:** Pic?

The reaction settles it — an explosion of excited squeaks and tight, happy
circles. He has a name before he has anything else.

## What he costs, and pays back

Naming him nearly gets him killed: the swarm that raised him tries to take him
back within the same scene, and Oyster beheads the one that grabbed him. Pic
never sees it happen.

> **CHIP:** He didn't see…

From there he stops being cargo. He fights beside them — biting, clawing,
opening his own ribcage in mimicry of a threat display he's too small to back
up — and it works often enough that Chip starts asking when he learned to do
that.

> **CHIP:** Since when can Pic fight like that?!
>
> **OYSTER (grinning):** He learns fast.

## Kin on both sides

He is terrified of the [Froststalkers](#/creatures/froststalkers) at first
sight and wins the [Froststalker King](#/characters/froststalker-king) over
anyway. He is also, it turns out, the missing child of the
[Piercer Queen](#/characters/piercer-queen) — a fact that costs the
Froststalker King his crown for a moment and nearly costs Pic everything when
she finds him.

> **PIERCER QUEEN:** You belong to me, child. Come home.

He stays. In the siege that follows, it's Pic's cry that vouches for the heroes
to a second Piercer swarm and turns an ambush into a rescue.

## What he's spared

He never sees who killed his mother, and he never sees
[Mach](#/characters/mach) do anything but lose. At the monument he trades blows
with an assassin twice his size — flame against blade, a stolen crossbow bolt
answered with fire down the leg — and comes out of it still small, still
following, still there when Oyster finally reaches the orb.

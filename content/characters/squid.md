The body of a man, the head of a squid, tendrils moving calmly in the water. He
is sitting on the steps of his own throne when Oyster and Chip finally reach it,
and he rises to greet them like a host.

> **SQUID:** Welcome, travelers. You've come far. Fought beasts. Faced fate. You
> may call me… Squid. Simple, yes. But powerful.

## What he claims

Everything.

> **SQUID:** I am the god of this world. I made the kings. The Bloom. The tides.
> The food chain.

He is also, by his own account, a prisoner. The [Bloom](#/characters/the-bloom)
— his own creation — locked him in the castle, took his power and ran the ocean
in his place. Oyster and Chip killing it is what lets him stand up.

He thanks them. He promises to revive the royals, restore the kingdoms, and
rebuild a peace that never quite existed. He says the Bloom will not be created
again.

Then his tone changes.

> **SQUID:** You cannot leave. Not with what you know.

## The theft

He becomes Oyster. A second Chip materialises beside him — an exact copy. He
explains it without any cruelty at all, which is what makes it unbearable.

> **SQUID:** I have watched your journey. Every decision. Every victory. Every
> death. I know how to be you. I will go to your Earth. Be your heroes. Take your
> ship. Take your names. You… will stay here. And rule this world in silence.

Four guards rise out of the shadows. The doors seal. The throne pulses purple.

> **SQUID:** The world is changing. But the throne never does.

## Read against the Bloom

The Bloom told Oyster the ocean was a cycle he could not win, and that he was
the final breath of it. Squid proves the Bloom right in a way the Bloom never
managed — the game does not end when the monster dies, it just changes hands.

The last thing Oyster and Chip hear is their own voices, walking away.

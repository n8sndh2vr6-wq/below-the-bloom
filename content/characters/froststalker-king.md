Massive, armoured, wreathed in icy mist, with luminous scars that pulse a faint
blue. He rules the [icy trenches](#/locations/icy-trenches) and the society built
inside them.

## First meeting

He arrives roaring, expecting to find his children dead and two intruders
standing over them. Instead the cubs are curled up asleep, breathing, and there
is a dead [Maw](#/creatures/maws) on the floor behind the strangers.

The roar stops mid-breath.

> **KING FROSTSTALKER:** Kindness from above… not for conquest. But to protect.
> That is rare. You are no longer prisoners here.

It is the hinge the whole chapter turns on. Everything before it says the ocean
is made of monsters. Everything after says the ocean is made of *nations*, and
that Oyster arrived in the middle of one without knowing.

## What he rules

Not a pack — a kingdom. Nests built into icy alcoves, work crews hauling
salvaged hull plating across frozen bridges, adults nudging cubs along and
growling encouragement. Ice bridges, glowing vents, patrols moving in order.

> **CHIP:** It's almost… civil. Like they've been building this for a long time.
> A society hidden in the deep.

## The alliance

He and the [Emergent King](#/characters/emergent-king) were enemies once. They
are not any more. Every year, when the currents shift and the Maw hunger rises,
the two kingdoms prepare together, because separately neither survives it.

He is also the one who tries to warn Oyster off the Bloom, dragging a spear
through a collapsed tunnel to do it.

> **FROSTSTALKER KING:** Do not listen to it! It bends the tide to its will — it
> poisons fate!

## The last crown standing

In [Scene 45](#/story/chapter-1/scene-45) he takes Oyster on his back and Chip
as a mounted cannon and rockets straight at the Bloom.

> **FROSTSTALKER KING:** Let us make history.

He is the only ruler alive at the end of it, and he knows exactly what that
costs.

> **FROSTSTALKER KING:** My kingdom… is all that's left. We are the top of the
> food chain now.

Then he turns for the dark with his army behind him, and leaves the survivors
with the closest thing to a blessing anyone offers them.

> **FROSTSTALKER KING:** Take care of the light, surface-dwellers.

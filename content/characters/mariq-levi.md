Twenties, jokester, optimist, and the crew's morale for as long as the crew
exists.

His opening line is a complaint filed in advance about the quality of alien
seafood.

> **MARIQ:** Alright, I'm just saying — if this planet's covered in ocean, there
> better be alien sushi down there. Or I'm filing a complaint.

He is scared of shrimp, and defends the position on principle:

> **MARIQ:** I'm not scared of shrimp. I just — look, anything with too many legs
> and no eyebrows? That's suspicious.

## The turn

He is also the one who says the thing that stops the room. The banter is still
going, everyone is still laughing, and Mariq goes quiet.

> **MARIQ:** Still gives me the creeps. Like something's… watching us already.

The lights flicker for half a second. The hum of the ship changes pitch.

He gets one more joke — *what's the worst that could happen, mutant krakens? evil
algae?* — and then the purple wave hits and he is trying to stabilise a ship
that is already being pulled down.

> **MARIQ:** I can't stabilize it — it's dragging us down—!

He does not survive the impact. He was, as it turns out, right about being
watched.

A small, sleek, glowing fish whose scales ripple like thoughts. It speaks
without moving its mouth. It is the antagonist of Chapter One and the reason
there is a Chapter One at all.

> **THE BLOOM:** I am the current, the tide, the teeth, the whisper between each
> breath in the dark. I do not command this world. I am this world.

## Reach

The violet light is not weather. Everything the pulse touches, it is steering.

- It brought down the research vessel in orbit. That is the
  [Bloom event](#/lore/the-bloom-event).
- The purple thread Oyster follows down through every layer of the ocean is the
  Bloom leading him.
- It halts a battlefield of thousands mid-strike, holds them suspended, and
  restarts them with a ripple of its fins.
- It raises the fallen with purple in their eyes — awake, and not in control.
- It splits into hundreds of identical clones carrying violet blades.

## Its philosophy

The Bloom describes the [food chain](#/lore/the-food-chain) as scripture rather
than ecology, and itself as the last clause.

> The Emergent rises. The Maw consumes. The Frost stalks. The Deadwake waits.
> And I… I decide.

Asked why it forces its kingdoms to tear each other apart, it does not deny the
cruelty. It reframes it.

> **THE BLOOM:** Because evolution does not pause for comfort. Because something
> greater is coming. And because death… is the only thing that makes life matter.

And on what power actually is:

> **THE BLOOM:** Power is not control. It is belief. When all eyes turn to you for
> answers, when the ocean holds its breath at your presence… that is power.

## The game

It offers Oyster rules, the way a bored god offers rules. Defeat a creature and
it joins your side. It gets worse as it goes. The kings come last.

> **THE BLOOM:** Win, and maybe I'll let you live. Lose, and… well, fate wins
> again.

It admits the motive out loud: it has not felt a proper smile in years, and what
it has learned joy is made of is *struggle, desperation, watching hope stretch
thin like gill-wire.*

## What it actually was

According to [Squid](#/characters/squid), the Bloom is not a god at all. It was
*made* — one of Squid's own creations — and it repaid that by stealing his power
and locking him inside his own castle.

It dies when the [Froststalker King](#/characters/froststalker-king) charges with
Oyster on his back and Chip as a cannon, and the shot puts it into the
[Emergent King](#/characters/emergent-king)'s mouth. It self-destructs rather
than be eaten.

Its last words are almost gentle.

> **BLOOM:** You've come far. Go to the castle now. End this. You've earned your
> peace.

It was lying about the peace.

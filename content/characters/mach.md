A shadow that follows the whole chapter before it ever speaks. First a shape
darting past a crumbled tower in [Scene 5](#/story/chapter-2/scene-05), then a
figure watching from across a sandy ruin in
[Scene 7](#/story/chapter-2/scene-07) — always gone by the time anyone looks
straight at it.

> **OYSTER:** Not gone. Waiting.

## Introduction by katana

He surfaces properly the instant the [Piercer Queen](#/characters/piercer-queen)
is dead, standing on her corpse to behead it, then answers Oyster's question
with nothing at all.

> **OYSTER:** Who… who are you?
>
> **SILHOUETTED FIGURE:** I'm here for reasons… very confidential.

Chip's lights catch him a moment later: black armor, a jagged hood, a breathing
mask, symbols pulsing faintly across his chest. Underneath it he looks human.
He answers that too, by cutting Chip's screen out and vanishing.

> **SILHOUETTED FIGURE:** Stay out of my way.

Chip's X-ray mode gets the rest of the truth later: a human skeleton under a
squid-like head, built slimmer and shorter than Oyster.

> **CHIP:** So… not Squid. Not you. Someone new.

## The name

He gives it up himself at the monument, katana already through Oyster's chest.

> **MACH:** Call me Mach — short for "Machete." I'm a bounty hunter, part of
> Squid's secret assassination order. Squid sent me… to kill you two. And I'm
> quite eager to finish the job… especially the little one.

He recognizes [Pic](#/characters/pic) on sight for what he is — an
Apex-Berserker Piercer — which suggests Mach knows considerably more about this
ocean than two survivors chasing a rumor do.

## The duel

He nearly wins. He gets through Chip's shield with thrown knives, shorts out
Chip's screen a second time, and puts a crossbow bolt into Oyster's shoulder.
What he doesn't survive is a straightforward punch — low, undignified, and
enough of an opening for Oyster to reach the orb.

A vial of pills saves him: an instant wave of ice that douses the fire Pic set
on his leg and the acid Oyster put into the fight. He vanishes in the frost and
reappears on the monument's spire, alive, watching from exactly where the
[Froststalker King](#/characters/froststalker-king) fell.

A fish slightly larger than the others, wearing a crown of coral and light, with
a melodic echoing voice and — by Chip's assessment — a smug royalty look.

> **CHIP:** The Emergent King. Has to be. He's wearing a crown. Also… kind of got
> that smug royalty look.

## What that understates

The [Emergents](#/creatures/emergents) are the oldest beings on the planet.
Their king speaks like something that has watched a very great deal and been
surprised by almost none of it, which is exactly what makes his respect for two
shipwrecked survivors worth something.

> **EMERGENT KING:** Many never return from Maw territory. Yet you live. You are
> resourceful.

## His kingdom

An aquatic palace lit bioluminescent-warm, where structures of coral, ice and
salvaged metal rise like a cityscape and visiting [Froststalkers](#/creatures/froststalkers)
move through the plazas without incident. It is the only place in the chapter
that looks like peace.

He earned it by ending a war. He and the [Froststalker King](#/characters/froststalker-king)
were enemies; now they prepare together each year for the Maw hunger, because
that is the only arrangement either kingdom survives.

## The warning

He is the one who tells Oyster what is underneath, and he does not soften it.

> **EMERGENT KING:** It will come sooner than expected. The pressure stirs. The
> deep trembles.

And when Oyster refuses to turn back anyway:

> **EMERGENT KING:** Then go with caution. You carry more than just survival. The
> ocean watches.

## How he dies

In the final battle the [Bloom](#/characters/the-bloom) is fired directly into
his open mouth. He bites down. The detonation takes him with it — the oldest
thing in the ocean spent as ordnance to kill the thing that had been running it.

> **EMERGENT KING (spirit):** The ocean owes you peace.

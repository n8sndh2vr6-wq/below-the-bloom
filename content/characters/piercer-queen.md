Wings outstretched, beak like a spear, white fur glowing faintly against the
dark of a Froststalker cave she has no business being in. She announces herself
before anyone sees her.

> **PIERCER QUEEN (O.S.):** My child is amongst you. Return him… or suffer the
> consequences.

## The claim

She isn't interested in a fight, at first. She's interested in
[Pic](#/characters/pic), and she is entirely sure of her right to him.

> **PIERCER QUEEN:** You belong to me, child. Come home. I made you. You're not
> one of them.

When the [Froststalker King](#/characters/froststalker-king) steps between
them, she breaks his crown and his ribs with one wing and doesn't slow down to
watch him fall.

> **PIERCER QUEEN (sneering):** Royalty? Not in my eyes.

Oyster and Chip's answer — that she'll have to go through them — earns
[Scene 27](#/story/chapter-2/scene-27)'s entire swarm as an answer of her own.

> **PIERCER QUEEN:** Kill them.

## How she loses

She wins most of it. One wingbeat's shockwave clears the battlefield of every
Froststalker still fighting, and she's a heartbeat from finishing Oyster, Chip
and Pic together when the [Maw King](#/characters/maw-king) hits her from the
side and puts a claw through her eye. Oyster finishes it from the one place she
can't reach him — inside her own open ribcage, with the acid blaster he's been
loading the whole fight.

> **OYSTER (quietly):** It's over.

## After

Her body is barely still before [Mach](#/characters/mach) beheads the corpse
without a word to anyone, atop it — the first sign in the chapter that Pic's
mother was not the worst thing watching him.

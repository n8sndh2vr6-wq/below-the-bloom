Orange eyes, mandibles, claws that click on coral, and a throne of black,
twisted, spiked coral at the centre of the [cavernous depths](#/locations/maw-territory).

He speaks fluent English, low and gravelled, and he is in no hurry at all.

## Not a beast

This is what makes him the chapter's worst thing. The [Froststalkers](#/creatures/froststalkers)
attack out of instinct and territory. The Maw King is neither mindless nor
starving. He is *entertained*.

> **MAW KING:** Should I end you swiftly… or savor your terror as I have done to
> others?

## The hand

He does not need Oyster's right hand. He takes it slowly, sawing through suit
and bone while Oyster screams into his own helmet speakers, and then holds it up
by the wrist to watch him writhe.

When Oyster kills two guards on adrenaline, the King applauds — genuinely
delighted — and lets him go.

> **MAW KING:** Splendid. Run, then — if you can.

Later, cornering him at a dead end:

> **MAW KING:** So young. So full of fight. But your kind always folds. Always
> breaks.

He gets a concentrated stream of compressed oxygen through the cheek for it.

## Conscripted

In the Bloom's arena he is beaten and, by the rules of the game, ends up
fighting *beside* Oyster — the boy whose hand is in his trophy pile.

It does not last. The Bloom takes control of him, walks him across the
battlefield, and uses him to cut the [Deadwake Queen](#/characters/deadwake-queen)
in half. Then it eats him.

## The apology

His spirit rises out of the water with the others, and says four words that
recontextualise everything he did.

> **MAW KING (spirit):** And defied the monster I once was.

Sixteen years old, lanky, and wearing a pressure suit unzipped at the collar
right up until the day it has to keep him alive.

Oyster is on the expedition because he read the files nobody was supposed to
read. The planet's numbers do not fit the rules, and that bothers him more than
it bothers any of the adults on the ship. When the Bloom takes the vessel down
and the crew with it, that same restlessness is the only thing that gets him out
of the airlock.

> **OYSTER:** We're not here just to explore. We're here because this planet
> doesn't fit the rules. The readings… they don't make sense.

## What he is not

He is not a soldier, and the story never pretends otherwise. Everything he wins
in Chapter One he wins badly — with a suit knife, a repurposed oxygen tool, a
fin torn off a corpse mid-fight, and a refusal to stop that reads as courage
from the outside and stubbornness from the inside.

He is also, repeatedly, the reason things go wrong. He stabs a glowing fish
because he is hungry and it turns out to be a baby [Emergent](#/creatures/emergents).
He cuts another and brings a [Deadwake](#/creatures/deadwakes) down on both of
them. The ocean keeps punishing him for being a boy who is tired and starving.

## The hand

In [Scene 21](#/story/chapter-1/scene-21) the [Maw King](#/characters/maw-king)
takes his right hand off at the wrist, slowly, because the slowness is the
point. Oyster kills two guards on rage alone and is allowed to run, since
running is better entertainment than dying.

Three scenes later [Chip](#/characters/chip) opens like a toolbox and gives him
a new one — pressure-sealed, neural-link capable, and fitted with a bottle
opener nobody asked for.

> **CHIP:** Welcome back, Two-Handed Terror.

## The choice that defines him

The [Deadwake Queen](#/characters/deadwake-queen) offers him a survivable deal:
lead five of her royal guards into the [Emergent kingdom](#/locations/emergent-kingdom)
and let them feed, or do not leave her throne room alive. He agrees, because
agreeing is the only way out of the room.

Then he stands in front of the two kings who trusted him and says it out loud
instead.

> **OYSTER:** They're here. Hiding behind your walls. Waiting to strike. And we
> were supposed to help them. But we won't. Prepare your armies. Now.

It starts a war, and it is the single most important thing he does.

## Where Chapter One leaves him

On a throne, in the dark, behind sealed doors — while something wearing his face
walks out of the castle with his name, his ship and his best friend's voice.

## Chapter Two: back out the doors

The castle's gates open on their own — Squid's doing, never explained — and
Oyster spends exactly two scenes breathing free air before deciding to go back
down. Squid mentioned "ancient powers" once, in passing, before revealing what
he really was. Oyster believes it anyway, and believes it's worth finding before
Squid does.

> **OYSTER:** That peace? It won't last if Squid ever comes back. We need to be
> ready.

He names [Pic](#/characters/pic) out of a swarm that nearly kills them both, and
the instinct to protect him surfaces every time it's tested — beheading the
Piercer that grabs him, throwing himself at a queen twice his size to stand
between her and her own child.

> **OYSTER:** If you want him… you'll have to go through us.

The rage has a ceiling this chapter, and it shows it when
[Mach](#/characters/mach) destroys Chip in front of him. Oyster drops every
defensive instinct he has and carves a path through an Acidic wave alone,
scaring Chip more than the enemy does.

> **CHIP:** Oyster! You can't—

He loses the [Froststalker King](#/characters/froststalker-king) at the
monument's spire, takes a katana through the chest from behind for his trouble,
and still reaches the orb — battered, one-upped by an assassin twice, and
finishing exactly what he came back down to finish.

> **ALL (in unison):** Squid… we'll find you.

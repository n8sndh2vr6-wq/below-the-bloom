Enormous, sleek and elegant, her bioluminescent fins pulsing dominance, on a
throne chiselled from obsidian coral at the heart of the
[Deadwake kingdom](#/locations/deadwake-kingdom).

Her voice is described as *smooth, regal, but sharp like a blade*, and it is the
only voice in the chapter that sounds like it is used to being obeyed.

## The offer

She does not kill Oyster and Chip when she easily could. She collects them,
because they are useful, and because they have already done something no prey
does — survived [Maw territory](#/locations/maw-territory).

> **DEADWAKE QUEEN:** You will be useful. You have made it further than most
> prey. You have fought Maws… killed one of my kin.

Her terms are simple and monstrous: lead five of her royal guards into the
[Emergent kingdom](#/locations/emergent-kingdom) and guide them to feast, or do
not leave.

When Oyster protests that these are the people who saved him, she gives the line
that defines her:

> **DEADWAKE QUEEN:** Do not confuse sentiment with survival.

## What makes her more than a villain

She is not wrong about the world she lives in. The [food chain](#/lore/the-food-chain)
really is the arrangement, the [annual war](#/lore/the-annual-war) really is
coming, and her kingdom really does have to eat. She offers a bargain rather
than a slaughter, which is more than the [Maw King](#/characters/maw-king) ever
did.

Betrayed in front of her enemies, her fury is personal:

> **DEADWAKE QUEEN:** YOU DARE DEFY ME?! I gave you a chance. And now… you drown
> with them!

## How she ends

Conscripted into the Bloom's game, beaten, she joins the side of the boy who
crossed her and fights the final battle at his back — tail crackling with venom,
diving in from above.

The Bloom puppets the Maw King and has him cut her in half. She goes out
grinning.

> **DEADWAKE QUEEN:** You'll need more… than that…

Her spirit is the first of the four to speak, and it is not bitter.

> **DEADWAKE QUEEN (spirit):** You held your ground, little ones.

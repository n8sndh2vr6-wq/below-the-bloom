Thirties, calm, assertive, and running the descent. She wants the crew loose and
laughing before the drop, and she says so.

> **DR. VOSS:** Honestly, this is good. I want this energy. After the last three
> expeditions? We deserve one that goes right.

That line is the entire character in miniature — someone who has already had
three bad ones and is trying to will this one into being different.

## Why she is here

She believes the structures under the ice mean intelligence, and that
intelligence means answers.

> **DR. VOSS:** That's why we're here. If there's intelligence down there… it
> changes everything. Maybe answers about the ones who came before us.

*The ones who came before us* is the only time anyone gestures at a wider history
in Chapter One, and she is dead four pages later.

## The crash

She is the first to see it — a pulse of purple light expanding off the planet's
surface like a ripple through space itself — and the only one who does anything
useful about it.

> **DR. VOSS:** We're losing altitude! I'm initiating the crash protocols —
> Oyster, Mariq, brace!

She does not survive the impact. Chip's scan afterwards finds the biosignatures
*gone, or way too faint*, and the two of them leave the wreck without ever
looking at the bodies.

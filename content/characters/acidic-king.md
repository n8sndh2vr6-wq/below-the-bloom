Six-legged, scarred, and dropped out of the dark hard enough to crack the
cavern floor, right as the group is finally in sight of
[the monument](#/locations/the-monument).

> **ACIDIC KING (voice booming):** All my troops… dead. Skywalkers crushed them.
> And you — threaten my species. I must kill you.

He is the only [Acidic](#/creatures/acidics) in the chapter who gets a name, a
voice, and a grievance instead of just a body count, and he is not wrong to have
one — by the time he shows up, his people have been fought to a standstill
across an entire siege and then trampled by [Skywalkers](#/creatures/skywalkers)
on top of it.

## The fight

He nearly wins alone. He coats Oyster's suit in poison until the seals fail,
drags him out of Chip's air bubble by the shoulder, knocks the
[Maw King](#/characters/maw-king) off a ledge, and shorts out Chip's systems
outright.

> **ACIDIC KING:** You think you win?

What turns it is [Pic](#/characters/pic) — a screech pitched straight into the
King's own echolocation, dropping him into a spasm just long enough for the
tide to shift.

## How he dies

Cornered and still fighting, he pummels toward Oyster for what should be the
last exchange. The [Froststalker King](#/characters/froststalker-king) gets
there first, launching them both up onto the monument's central spire together.
Neither of them gets up.

> The King's sacrifice is instant and fatal — the Acidic King's body falls limp.

He is the second king this chapter kills, and the first one nobody mourns.
